**How to use this guide:**

- Find the topic, then open the file whose name starts with the listed FIE page number. When a document was produced twice, the earlier number comes first and the later copy follows in brackets.
- **★** = new information or worth opening.
- **(ours)** = material we wrote or sent, coming back from Farmers' file.

**Citing:** about 457 of the 961 Oct. 21 pages are re-copies of Sept. 25 documents under new page numbers. Pick one number per document and use it consistently. The earlier (Sept. 25) number is the natural choice. The Sept. 25 copies sometimes also keep better original file properties: Brau's photo file, for example, still shows it was made on an iPhone.

## At a glance

- **About half of the Oct. 21 production repeats the Sept. 25 production.** Much of the rest is letters Farmers sent us, our own emails and submissions copied into Farmers' claim mailbox, vendor reports, and two bundles Farmers sent the appraisal panel. Those bundles are themselves mostly copies.
- **There are almost no internal Farmers discussions.** Nearly every "Farmers Internal Email" is an adjuster forwarding *our* email into the claim-document mailbox. There are no supervisor discussions, peer reviews, claim notes, or decision records.
- **The genuinely new or useful items** are:
  - Farmers' own weather reports (Benchmark)
  - aerial and measurement reports (EagleView, Hover)
  - Brau's Sept. 6, 2024 iPhone photos with his own captions
  - Brau's $0 Xactimate estimate
  - Farmers' answers to the Commerce Department and the Attorney General
  - Farmers' instruction letters to the appraisal panel
  - the "appraiser's position" emails

## Cautions for using these documents

- **Brau's photos exist and have his captions,** so "Farmers has no record of Brau's observations" is too strong. The accurate point is that his notes, measurements, any report and the original photo files are missing. None of his captions that we reviewed mentions exterior hail damage; the few captions are mostly elevation labels plus the interior-water note at FIE 0718 [1400].
- **Benchmark cuts both ways.** It undercuts Prieve's "no hail after 2020" premise, but its date-of-loss report is Farmers' best causation evidence against everything except the two awarded windows.
- **The Aug. 19, 2024 "DamageDetails" document (0754–0758) is only a modest point.** It shows Farmers had our statement of exterior hail damage before the Hancock inspection and the Sept. 3 denial. But its photos are mostly interior staining, which fits Farmers' framing that the claim began as an interior leak.
- **The prior-claim files were given to the panel** (Bundle 2). Farmers will argue the panel weighed prior damage when it picked two windows.
- **J6944** excludes matching costs caused by "obsolescence or discontinuation" for windows; the buy-back applies to siding and roof only.
