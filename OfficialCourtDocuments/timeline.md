# Case Timeline — Lu & Hu v. Fire Insurance Exchange

**Court:** Hennepin County District Court, Fourth Judicial District
**File No.:** 27-CV-26-11606 · **Case Type:** Contract · **Judge:** Jamie L. Anderson
**Underlying loss:** Hail damage, 7/13/2024 · **Plaintiffs:** Yibiao Lu and Jie Hu (pro se)

This is a brief index of documents filed in the court file, in date order. It is a
navigation aid, not a substitute for reading the filings themselves.

---

## 07/08/2026 — Summons
Civil Summons directed to Fire Insurance Exchange, commencing the action and giving
the standard 21-day deadline to serve an Answer. Filed with the original Complaint.

## 07/09/2026 — Notice of Case Assignment
Court administration assigns the case to District Court Judge Jamie L. Anderson for
all further proceedings.

## 07/13/2026 — Notice of Noncompliance (Minn. Gen. R. Prac. 11) — two copies
Court administrator notifies each plaintiff separately (one addressed to Jie Hu, one to
Yibiao Lu) that the Complaint contains restricted identifiers. Plaintiffs have 3 days to
refile a redacted Complaint with Form 11.1 (CON111), or the document may be stricken.
The notice also states that a resubmitted document is filed as of its resubmission date
unless the original filing date is restored **by motion** filed within 3 days.

## 07/20/2026 — Corrected Complaint (Complaint-Civil)
Refiled 46-page Complaint with restricted identifiers redacted. Claims: breach of the
homeowner's policy; declaratory judgment under Minn. Stat. ch. 555; a declaration that
the 9/12/2025 appraisal award — not itemized as Minn. Stat. § 65A.01 requires — does not
preclude the contract claim as to items it did not itemize (alternatively, modification
or vacatur of the award); and Minnesota Consumer Fraud Act claims (§§ 325F.69, 325F.70
subd. 3, 8.31 subd. 3a). Jury trial demanded.

## 07/20/2026 — Notice of Motion and Motion for Relation-Back of Filing Date
Plaintiffs move under Minn. Gen. R. Prac. 11.05 to have the corrected Complaint deemed
filed as of 7/8/2026, the original filing date. Decision requested on written submissions
without a hearing (Gen. R. Prac. 115.04).

## 07/20/2026 — [Proposed] Order
Plaintiffs' draft order submitted with the relation-back motion, which if signed would
deem the corrected Complaint filed as of 7/8/2026 for all purposes. **This is not a court
order** — it is unsigned, the date line is blank, and it was submitted by plaintiffs. The
only signed order in the file to date is the 7/28/2026 Order Enlarging Time for Answer.

## 07/27/2026 — Affidavit of Service (Plaintiffs)
Yibiao Lu attests that on 7/20/2026 he served the corrected Complaint and the
relation-back motion with proposed order on Fire Insurance Exchange c/o Corporation
Service Company, Roseville MN, by U.S. certified mail.

## 07/28/2026 — Notice of Appearance (Defendant)
Kennedy Law Firm P.C. (Kevin J. Kennedy, Mary J. Baskfield), Woodbury MN, appears for
Fire Insurance Exchange.

## 07/28/2026 — Correspondence for Judicial Approval (Defendant)
Letter from Baskfield to Judge Anderson requesting an extension to answer under Minn.
R. Civ. P. 6.02. Asserts service under Minn. Stat. § 45.028 was not effective until the
Affidavit of Compliance was filed 7/8/2026, making the answer due 7/29/2026, and that
plaintiffs declined a voluntary extension. Requests an extension to 8/10/2026.

## 07/28/2026 — Order Enlarging Time for Answer
Judge Anderson grants the request for cause shown, citing the 46-page complaint and the
noncompliance/refiling sequence. **Answer due no later than 8/10/2026.**

## 08/10/2026 — Defendant's Answer
Fire Insurance Exchange answers. Core position: the binding 9/12/2025 appraisal award
identified hail as the cause of loss for **two** windows, with RCV $12,800 and ACV
$6,400 — below the $17,720 wind/hail deductible — so nothing is owed. Denies that hail
damaged the residence on 7/13/2024 while admitting the award found hail damaged two
windows. Alleges plaintiffs limited the inspection scope. Three affirmative defenses:
Rule 12 (jurisdiction over FIE / failure to state a claim), the appraisal award is
binding, and a general denial. Jury demanded. No counterclaim.

## 08/10/2026 — Affidavit of Service (Defendant)
Julie Parks attests to serving the Answer and affidavit of service on plaintiffs by U.S.
mail, with a cover letter from Baskfield also sent by email.
