## 07/09/2026 — Notice of Case Assignment
Court administration assigns the case to District Court Judge Jamie L. Anderson for

## 07/13/2026 — Notice of Noncompliance (Minn. Gen. R. Prac. 11) — two copies
Court administrator notifies each plaintiff separately (one addressed to Jie Hu, one to
Yibiao Lu) that the Complaint contains restricted identifiers. Plaintiffs have 3 days to
refile a redacted Complaint with Form 11.1 (CON111), or the document may be stricken.
The notice also states that a resubmitted document is filed as of its resubmission date
unless the original filing date is restored **by motion** filed within 3 days.

## 07/20/2026 — Corrected Complaint (Complaint-Civil)
Refiled 46-page Complaint with restricted identifiers redacted. Claims: breach of the
homeowner's policy; declaratory judgment under Minn. Stat. ch. 555; a declaration that
the 9/12/2025 appraisal award — not itemized as Minn. Stat. § 65A.01 requires — does not
preclude the contract claim as to items it did not itemize (alternatively, modification
or vacatur of the award); and Minnesota Consumer Fraud Act claims (§§ 325F.69, 325F.70
subd. 3, 8.31 subd. 3a). Jury trial demanded.

## 07/20/2026 — Notice of Motion and Motion for Relation-Back of Filing Date
Plaintiffs move under Minn. Gen. R. Prac. 11.05 to have the corrected Complaint deemed
filed as of 7/8/2026, the original filing date. Decision requested on written submissions
without a hearing (Gen. R. Prac. 115.04).

## 07/20/2026 — [Proposed] Order
Plaintiffs' draft order submitted with the relation-back motion, which if signed would

## 07/28/2026 — Correspondence for Judicial Approval (Defendant)
Letter from Baskfield to Judge Anderson requesting an extension to answer under Minn.

## 07/28/2026 — Order Enlarging Time for Answer
Judge Anderson grants the request for cause shown, citing the 46-page complaint and the
noncompliance/refiling sequence. **Answer due no later than 8/10/2026.**

## 08/10/2026 — Defendant's Answer
Fire Insurance Exchange answers. Core position: the binding 9/12/2025 appraisal award
identified hail as the cause of loss for **two** windows, with RCV $12,800 and ACV
$6,400 — below the $17,720 wind/hail deductible — so nothing is owed. Denies that hail
damaged the residence on 7/13/2024 while admitting the award found hail damaged two
windows. Alleges plaintiffs limited the inspection scope. Three affirmative defenses:
Rule 12 (jurisdiction over FIE / failure to state a claim), the appraisal award is
binding, and a general denial. Jury demanded. No counterclaim.

## 08/10/2026 — Affidavit of Service (Defendant)
Julie Parks attests to serving the Answer and affidavit of service on plaintiffs by U.S.
mail, with a cover letter from Baskfield also sent by email.

## 08/28/2026 — Correspondence for Judicial Approval (Defendant)
Letter from Baskfield to Judge Anderson, filed 3:15 PM, reporting that the Rule 26.06
discovery conference was held that day and the parties could not agree on a joint
discovery plan. Frames the disagreement as being about **dates only**: defendant proposed
trial 9/1/2027 with discovery complete 2/19/2027; plaintiffs proposed a trial-ready date
of June 2028 with fact discovery closing 8/31/2027. Requests a scheduling conference.
The letter does not mention the parties' other open disputes (ESI form and metadata,
privilege log and claw-back procedure, protective-order tiers).

## 09/01/2026 — Correspondence for Judicial Approval (Defendant)

folder. The 9/7 date supersedes the Rule 26.06(b) default of 9/11/2026. **9/7/2026 is

## 09/01/2026 — Defendant's Proposed Rule 26.06 Discovery Plan
Defendant's unilateral plan, filed with the 9/1 cover letter. Recites that the parties
conferred 8/28/2026 and could not agree. Positions on the six Rule 26.06(b) subjects:

- **¶1 Initial disclosures** — no changes; per Rule 26.01.
- **¶2 Subjects/completion/phasing** — discovery on "Plaintiff's claims and Defendant's
  defenses"; no phasing, no issue limits.
- **¶3 ESI** — parties agree to retain relevant documents including ESI; "have not
  identified any issues"; may meet later as the need arises. No production format, no
  native/metadata provision.
- **¶4 Privilege** — follow Rule 26. No privilege-log protocol, no claw-back agreement.
- **¶5 Limits** — none beyond the rules.
- **¶6 Rule 16.02 deadlines** — initial disclosures 9/25/2026; discovery deadline
  2/19/2027; plaintiffs' expert disclosures 3/15/2027; defendant's 4/17/2027; rebuttal
  5/14/2027; non-dispositive motions heard by 3/1/2027; dispositive motions heard by
  4/16/2027; mediation by 6/1/2027; pretrial 8/11/2027; trial 9/1/2027.

Changes from the version exchanged before the 8/28 conference: the past-date errors are
gone (non-dispositive motions moved from "March 1, 2026" to 3/1/2027; mediation from
"June 1, 2026" to 6/1/2027). The internal date conflicts remain:

- The **discovery deadline (2/19/2027) precedes all three expert-disclosure dates**
  (3/15, 4/17, 5/14/2027), leaving no window for expert depositions or expert-related
  discovery motions.
- **Dispositive motions heard by 4/16/2027 precedes the 5/14/2027 rebuttal expert
  deadline** and falls 1 day before defendant's own expert disclosures would be complete
  under ¶6 — summary judgment would be briefed on an incomplete expert record.
- **Non-dispositive motions heard by 3/1/2027** requires filing by ~2/8/2027 under Gen.
  R. Prac. 115.04 — before the 2/19/2027 discovery close, so no motion to compel on
  end-of-discovery production is possible.
- ¶3 and ¶4 assert the parties "have not identified any issues" and agree to follow the
  rules. Plaintiffs' 8/28 redline had put ESI native-format/metadata, a privilege log,
  claw-back, and a single-tier protective order in dispute. Preserve that record.
