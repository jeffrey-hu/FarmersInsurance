## 08/10/2026 — Defendant's Answer
Fire Insurance Exchange answers. Core position: the binding 9/12/2025 appraisal award
identified hail as the cause of loss for **two** windows, with RCV $12,800 and ACV
$6,400 — below the $17,720 wind/hail deductible — so nothing is owed. Denies that hail
damaged the residence on 7/13/2024 while admitting the award found hail damaged two
windows. Alleges plaintiffs limited the inspection scope. Three affirmative defenses:
Rule 12 (jurisdiction over FIE / failure to state a claim), the appraisal award is
binding, and a general denial. Jury demanded. No counterclaim.

## 09/01/2026 — Defendant's Proposed Rule 26.06 Discovery Plan
Defendant's unilateral plan, filed with the 9/1 cover letter. Recites that the parties
conferred 8/28/2026 and could not agree. Positions on the six Rule 26.06(b) subjects:

- **¶1 Initial disclosures** — no changes; per Rule 26.01.
- **¶2 Subjects/completion/phasing** — discovery on "Plaintiff's claims and Defendant's
  defenses"; no phasing, no issue limits.
- **¶3 ESI** — parties agree to retain relevant documents including ESI; "have not
  identified any issues"; may meet later as the need arises. No production format, no
  native/metadata provision.
- **¶4 Privilege** — follow Rule 26. No privilege-log protocol, no claw-back agreement.
- **¶5 Limits** — none beyond the rules.
- **¶6 Rule 16.02 deadlines** — initial disclosures 9/25/2026; discovery deadline
  2/19/2027; plaintiffs' expert disclosures 3/15/2027; defendant's 4/17/2027; rebuttal
  5/14/2027; non-dispositive motions heard by 3/1/2027; dispositive motions heard by
  4/16/2027; mediation by 6/1/2027; pretrial 8/11/2027; trial 9/1/2027.

Changes from the version exchanged before the 8/28 conference: the past-date errors are
gone (non-dispositive motions moved from "March 1, 2026" to 3/1/2027; mediation from
"June 1, 2026" to 6/1/2027). The internal date conflicts remain:

- The **discovery deadline (2/19/2027) precedes all three expert-disclosure dates**
  (3/15, 4/17, 5/14/2027), leaving no window for expert depositions or expert-related
  discovery motions.
- **Dispositive motions heard by 4/16/2027 precedes the 5/14/2027 rebuttal expert
  deadline** and falls 1 day before defendant's own expert disclosures would be complete
  under ¶6 — summary judgment would be briefed on an incomplete expert record.
- **Non-dispositive motions heard by 3/1/2027** requires filing by ~2/8/2027 under Gen.
  R. Prac. 115.04 — before the 2/19/2027 discovery close, so no motion to compel on
  end-of-discovery production is possible.
- ¶3 and ¶4 assert the parties "have not identified any issues" and agree to follow the
  rules. Plaintiffs' 8/28 redline had put ESI native-format/metadata, a privilege log,
  claw-back, and a single-tier protective order in dispute. Preserve that record.

Note: the signature block gives Baskfield's attorney number as **#314572**; the 7/28/2026
Notice of Appearance and prior filings use **#354172**. Verify which is correct before
citing it anywhere.

## 09/04/2026 — Plaintiffs' Proposed Discovery Plan
Plaintiffs' unilateral Rule 26.06 plan, 6 pages, dated and filed 9/4/2026 — three days
ahead of the Court's 9/7 deadline, which fell on Labor Day. This is the V5 draft
(`Filings/Discovery/discoveryPlanAndInformationalStatement/Plaintiffs_Rule_26.06_Discovery_Plan_V5.md`);
the copy as filed is in `Filings/Discovery/CommunicationLogs/2026-09-04-OurProposal/`.

Opens with a **"Scope of the parties' disagreement"** paragraph correcting the record.
Defendant's 8/28 letter described the impasse as "significantly different positions on
proposed dates"; the plan states that plaintiffs also proposed substantive ESI, privilege
and protective-order terms, that defendant's plan does not adopt them, and that — because
defendant's ¶¶3–4 are written in the joint voice — plaintiffs have **not** agreed to those
paragraphs or to the deadlines in its ¶6.

Positions on the Rule 26.06(c) subjects:

- **¶1 Initial disclosures** — no changes; 9/25/2026, the one date both sides state.
- **¶2 Subjects/completion/phasing** — no phasing, no issue limits; fact discovery precedes
  expert discovery. Ten enumerated subjects (causation and location of the 7/13/2024 damage
  including components the panel never evaluated; the three inspections — Hancock 8/29/2024,
  Brau 9/6/2024, Prieve File #241008 — and the decision to limit Prieve to windows; the
  9/3 and 11/4/2024 denials and the engineer requirement; the 2020 claim and Xactimate file;
  the 9/12/2025 award and the appraiser/umpire files; internal communications and recorded
  calls; claims-handling policies and third-party-report review practice; vendor retention
  and oversight; the audit trail and record retention, including the lost 9/6/2024 inspection
  record; and the response to the 11/19/2024 Dept. of Commerce complaint, ID 93547). A
  "Basis for the proposed fact-discovery period" paragraph grounds the length in three
  specific gaps: the unidentified 2020 damage locations, defendant's two irreconcilable
  accounts of the 9/6/2024 inspection (10/10/2025 "[t]he file does not contain a report on
  the requested date" vs. 10/29/2025 "[a]ll the information we can release has already been
  sent to you"), and the award's undefined item codes "F 1.7" and "B 1.2."
- **¶3 ESI** — native format with metadata intact or another reasonably usable agreed form;
  searchability and metadata not to be degraded; no printing to image or flattening; applies
  in particular to the photographs from all three inspections.
- **¶4 Privilege** — Rule 26, plus a privilege log under Rule 26.02(f) and a non-waiver /
  claw-back agreement **entered as a court order** so its protections bind non-parties.
- **¶5 Limits** — none beyond the Rules, reserving the right to confer on additional
  deposition hours for a Rule 30.02(f) organizational deposition.
- **¶6 Confidentiality** — a single-tier CONFIDENTIAL protective order: **no
  attorneys'-eyes-only** tier (plaintiffs are pro se and it would bar them from the evidence
  in their own case); no designation merely because a document came from a claim file; and
  named access for **Jeffrey Hu**, who assists plaintiffs, subject to a signed written
  undertaking. Defendant, as the party seeking protection, is expected to propose the form.
- **¶7 Rule 16.02 deadlines** — initial disclosures 9/25/2026; substantial completion of
  document production 2/26/2027; amend/join 3/31/2027; fact discovery closes 6/30/2027;
  non-dispositive motions heard by 8/13/2027; plaintiffs' experts 9/24/2027; defendant's
  10/25/2027; § 604.18 motion to amend filed by 11/1/2027; rebuttal experts 11/22/2027; ADR
  completed 12/10/2027; expert discovery closes 1/14/2028; dispositive motions heard by
  3/3/2028; **trial-ready 5/15/2028** — a fixed date, not one tied to a dispositive ruling.
  Five notes explain the amendment sequencing (tied to defendant's jurisdiction defense and
  the attorney-in-fact documents), the separate § 604.18 deadline (subd. 4 bars pleading bad
  faith in the original complaint), the placement of non-dispositive motions before expert
  disclosures so a compelled production is still useful, the overall sequencing, and the
  fixed trial-ready date.
- **¶8 Scheduling information** — jury trial (both sides demanded one); non-binding mediation
  under Gen. R. Prac. 114.02(c)(1) with a neutral experienced in insurance-coverage or
  construction-defect disputes; retained experts on hail causation/damage plus valuation;
  **4–5 trial days**; no related cases (the Dept. of Commerce complaint is not a pending
  court case).

The plan expressly **shortens plaintiffs' own proposal**: the trial-ready date moves from
the June 2028 offered at the 8/28 conference to 5/15/2028, achieved by placing ADR after the
exchange of expert reports rather than before, and by compressing each expert round to about
one month. That is a move toward defendant's position, made on the record.
