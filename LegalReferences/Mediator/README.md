## Standing reminders

- **Verify before filing (Order ¶13).** The roster changes; re-pull it before the 10/27 notice.
- **Ask, don't allege.** Rule 114.13 subd. 3 entitles you to a written statement of qualifications from anyone serving for compensation — that is the proper way to resolve any roster-status question.
- **If the chosen neutral is off-roster**, write agreement to the Rule 114.13 Code of Ethics into the engagement agreement. It restores by contract most of what roster status would have given, and costs nothing.
- **Costs are split equally by ¶6.** Ask every candidate for four numbers: hourly rate, minimum block, preparation time, and the cancellation policy.
