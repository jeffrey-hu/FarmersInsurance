## 3. Why the second sentence was locked too

The Policy's **Limited water coverage** extension (Section I – Extensions of Coverage) covers "hail, rain, snow, or sleet entering through an opening in the roof or wall" **only if the opening was first caused by** fire, lightning, explosion, riot or civil commotion, aircraft or vehicles, vandalism, collapse, falling objects, or **windstorm**. **Hail is not on that list.** Defendant's sentence tracks that trigger language, which means it was positioning against the water-intrusion claim in July 2025. Relevant to the consequential water-intrusion and mold row in Section C of Plaintiffs' Initial Disclosures, which is pleaded under Count IV (Complaint ¶ 71(c)) rather than as a policy benefit.
