## 1. The problem in one paragraph

`2025-09-18-PostAppraisalEmailChain.md` is an excellent working compilation of seventeen post-appraisal communications between September 18 and October 29, 2025. It is **not evidence**. It is a document Plaintiffs created, it paraphrases in places, and for some entries it summarizes rather than reproduces. Two of the entries — the **October 10, 2025** Farmers response and the **October 29, 2025** Brau reply — are the factual core of the missing-Brau-notes argument, which your own strategy files identify as the strongest adverse-inference candidate in the case. Neither exists in the project as an original.

Compare the July 2025 exchange, which was preserved correctly as three PDFs. The October emails are the ones that matter most and the ones least well preserved.

## 3. What to do, in order

**1. Export the originals from the email account — this week.** For every Farmers communication from September 18 through October 29, 2025, and for the February 2025 exchanges:

- Save each as a **PDF including the full header block** (From, To, Cc, Date with time and time zone, Subject), or export as `.eml`/`.msg`, which preserves headers natively. A screenshot of the message body is the weakest form — it loses the headers that make the message self-authenticating.
- Do **not** edit, redact, or re-type. Export as received.
- Name them on the existing convention: `2025-10-10-FarmersResponse-NoReportOnRequestedDate.pdf`, `2025-10-29-BrauResponse-AllInformationWeCanRelease.pdf`.
- File them in `EventDocuments/` alongside the July 2025 PDFs, and leave the compilation `.md` in place as an index.

**2. Check for the voicemail.** If the February 10, 2025, 11:06 a.m. voicemail audio still exists on the phone or in a voicemail-to-email archive, export it now. If only a transcription exists, note who transcribed it and when — a party's own transcript is not self-authenticating, which is why the RFA in §4 asks FIE to admit the **content** rather than to authenticate a transcript.

**3. Do not alter the compilation to fill gaps.** `2025-09-18-PostAppraisalEmailChain.md` is useful precisely because it is a contemporaneous working document. Adding to it now, after litigation began, weakens it. Add the originals as separate files instead.

**4. Preserve on your side too.** The litigation-hold obligation runs both ways. Do not delete anything from the email account, and turn off any auto-delete or auto-archive rule that could reach 2024–2025 mail.

## 4. The discovery fix — do not rely on your own copies

Two independent routes recover these documents from FIE, which is better than proving up your own copies:

**Requests for Admission — authenticate.** See `../Filings/Discovery/RequestForAdmission/FirstSet/Proposed_Changes_RFA_First_Set_2026-09-12.md`, items **A-1 through A-4**. Rule 36 is uncapped, and under Rule 36.02 an admission is conclusively established for the action. If Plaintiffs' copies cannot be authenticated any other way, FIE's admission substitutes for the document.

**Requests for Production — get FIE's copies.** Request 36 as proposed (item P-25) seeks "all audio recordings, voicemail messages, transcripts, call notes, and telephone logs relating to The Claim," including the February 3 call and the February 10 voicemail, together with FIE's retention policy. Request 1 (the entire claim file) and Request 20 (the claims-portal record) should produce the October 2025 emails from FIE's side.

**If FIE's copies do not come back either**, that absence is itself evidence — and it converts a documentation gap into the same Rule 37 argument the missing Brau notes support.

Nothing here is unfixable, and most of it is a half-hour in the email client. But it should be done **before** the discovery sets go out, so that if FIE's production is thin you already know what you hold and what you do not.
