## 2. Why trim at all

Rule 36 has no numerical cap, but Rule 26.02(b) lets the Court "limit… the number of requests under Rule 36," and 200-plus requests served days after 53 RFPs is the fact pattern a burden objection is built on. A blanket objection costs Farmers one letter; it costs Plaintiffs a meet-and-confer, a three-page letter and a conference call — weeks that matter against the **November 16, 2026** joinder deadline. The 9/19 update took 25 requests out and added 24, so the volume is essentially unchanged. The test applied here: **do Plaintiffs lose anything by not asking this now?** For the 21 below, the answer is no.
