## 1. Bottom line

The set is strong, and most of it can be served as written. But **four requests are factually wrong on the face of FIE's own documents**, and three more are worded so that FIE can deny them truthfully. Under Minn. R. Civ. P. 37.03(b)(1) and (3), a request that FIE can truthfully deny carries no fee-shifting consequence. Fix these before service. They are text edits, not new research.

| # | Blocking defect | What the source actually shows |
|---|---|---|
| **3** | Calls hail an "enumerated covered peril under Section I (Loss or Damage Insured)." | **Wrong.** The Next Generation form is an *open-perils* grant ("We insure accidental direct physical loss or damage…"). "26. Windstorm or hail" appears only in **Section I – B. Excluded Causes of Loss or Damage**, with narrow carve-outs (sand/dust in personal property, watercraft, awnings/towers). Hail is not "enumerated" as a covered peril anywhere. FIE can deny truthfully. (The same wording is in Complaint ¶10.) |
| **23** | "Greg Tomes did not sign The Appraisal Award." | **Wrong as worded.** On the award image, Tomes **did** sign and date (9-12-2025) the **second appraiser line of the upper block**, which acknowledges that Wade Roos "has been appointed to act as umpire." Tomes left blank only his line under **"We certify the amounts established above for the described loss,"** and that line has no date. FIE can deny the request as drafted. This was the one open "verify before serving" item, and the check fails. |
| **51** | The 2020 Xactimate has "no window-related line item other than the line items approving two casement windows." | **Wrong.** Estimate YIBIAO_LU2 (2/18/2022) contains four window-related lines: **1** (R&R wood casement ×2), **2** (window blind detach & reset), **3** (retrofit-window charge), and **32** ("Window treatment repair," a labor minimum). |
| **21** | Asks FIE to admit the award "is binding upon" FIE "as to the matters it determined." | **Already admitted, and it helps FIE.** Answer ¶48: *"FIE admits that the Appraisal Award is binding."* This request restates Affirmative Defense 2. Replace it with a narrower causation lock (see §4, U-6). |

**The most important request missing from the set is the marring carve-back for hail.** RFA 34 establishes that FIE's "Marring" definition includes "denting." On its own, that helps FIE. The policy's Marring provision (Section I.A.8) goes on: *"However, we do insure marring which is directly caused by, arises out of or results from … b. windstorm or hail."* FIE's November 4, 2024 Coverage Outcome Attachment cites "8. Marring." and then shows an ellipsis, so the hail carve-back is not reproduced. That omission is an admission to lock down now. It bears on the exclusion defense and on the Consumer Fraud Act count. See §4, U-1.

**Two strategic risks the file does not flag:**

1. **RFA 63 (Semco ceased operations) points FIE toward endorsement J6944.** The 2023 Declarations list **J6944 1st ed.**, "Limited Matching of Undamaged Property." It excludes matching costs caused by **"obsolescence or discontinuation."** Semco's closure is exactly that fact. The project already holds matching requests until FIE raises J6944 itself. **Hold No. 63 with Second Set Part 2** for the same reason. (Note: RFP 24, served 9/15, already names J6944 in a document request. So FIE knows the endorsement exists, but nothing yet connects it to the Semco discontinuation. No. 63 would make that connection for FIE.)
2. **Pending proposals A-6 [41], [42] and [44] would signal defenses FIE has not pleaded** (limitations and suit-limitation; 2020 setoff). The corrected Complaint was filed 7/20/2026. The policy requires suit within two years, and the loss was 7/13/2024. The Scheduling Order sets **no deadline for amending pleadings**. FIE's Answer pleads no limitations defense. **Do not ask about it.** Instead, lock in the date service became effective, using FIE's own counsel's statement (§4, U-2).

**Urgent additions tied to the November 16, 2026 joinder deadline or to preserving evidence** are in §4: U-2 (service and jurisdiction), U-3 ("acted on behalf of FIE" for each adjuster), and U-4 (Brau's notes and the litigation hold).
