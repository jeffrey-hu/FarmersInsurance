Re: Lu and Hu v. Fire Insurance Exchange, Court File No. 27-CV-26-11606 (Your File No. 5490): Plaintiffs' First Set of Requests for Admission

Counsel:

We write in response to the part of your September 25, 2026 letter concerning Plaintiffs' First Set of Requests for Admission, served September 20, 2026. The other matters in your letter will be addressed separately.

This case is not limited to the appraisal award. The Complaint also pleads breach of the insurance contract, declaratory relief on coverage, and claims under the Minnesota Consumer Fraud Act, and each request relates to those claims or to Defendant's defenses. Defendant's own Rule 26.06 Discovery Plan, filed September 1, 2026, states at paragraph 5 that "[t]he parties do not recommend any limitations on discovery beyond those set forth in the Minnesota Rules of Civil Procedure," and Rule 36 sets no limit on the number of requests for admission. We therefore do not agree to limit the Requests for Admission to 25, and we do not agree that Defendant may select them.

To narrow what remains, Plaintiffs ask Defendant to answer the following requests within the time Rule 36.01 provides, which we calculate as on or about October 21, 2026: **Nos. 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 29, 30, 33, 34, 35, 43, 47, 49, 55, 56, 59, 60, 69, 70, 72, 73, 79, 80, 81, 82, 83, 84, 85, 86, 94, 113, 114, 115, 128, 129, 135 and 140.**

Your letter acknowledges that "Fire Insurance Exchange is a proper party to this lawsuit," and Defendant's First Affirmative Defense pleads "a lack of jurisdiction over Defendant FIE." Requests Nos. 113, 114, 115, 128 and 129 address those two points directly. If Defendant serves signed answers admitting each of those five requests, without qualification, by October 9, 2026, Plaintiffs will on receipt withdraw Requests **Nos. 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 88, 89, 90, 91, 92, 112, 116, 117, 121, 125, 126, 127 and 137**, which go to the same subjects. Until then those requests remain pending and are to be answered with the rest of the set. We offer this so that the entity and jurisdiction questions can be closed before the November 16, 2026 joinder deadline without further correspondence or a motion.

All other Requests are withdrawn, without prejudice to serving requests on the same subjects later in discovery.

Service on Plaintiffs should continue to be made to both bill_y_lu@yahoo.com and jie_h_hu@yahoo.com.
