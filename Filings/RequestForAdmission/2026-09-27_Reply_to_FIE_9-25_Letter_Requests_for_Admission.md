Counsel:

We write in response to the part of your September 25, 2026 letter concerning Plaintiffs' First Set of Requests for Admission, served September 20, 2026. The other matters in your letter will be addressed separately.

This case is not limited to the appraisal award. The Complaint also pleads breach of the insurance contract, declaratory relief on coverage, and claims under the Minnesota Consumer Fraud Act, and each request relates to those claims or to Defendant's defenses.
