# Intro / Engagement Email to Candidate Expert

**How to use:** Send this as a first-contact email to a candidate forensic engineer or meteorologist. Keep the first email brief — enough to run a conflict check and scope the work, without sharing strategy. Fill the brackets. Attach nothing yet; send the record only after they clear conflicts and you decide to engage. (Not legal advice.)

---

**Subject:** Forensic [engineering / meteorology] expert inquiry — hail-damage insurance case (Hennepin County, MN)

Dear [Dr./Mr./Ms. Last Name],

My spouse and I are the plaintiffs, appearing pro se, in a first-party homeowner's insurance case in Hennepin County District Court, Minnesota (*Lu and Hu v. Fire Insurance Exchange*, No. 27-CV-26-11606). Our home in Eden Prairie sustained hail damage on July 13–14, 2024, and we are looking for a qualified forensic [structural/building-envelope engineer / meteorologist] to evaluate causation and, potentially, to serve as a testifying expert.

In brief: our insurer's retained engineer concluded the damage was not hail-related, while the case's binding appraisal award found hail as the cause of loss to two windows. We believe hail damaged many additional windows and exterior components (downspouts, flashing, trim) that were never evaluated. We need an independent expert to assess whether the July 2024 hail caused that damage and to evaluate the opposing engineer's methodology, and — separately — to establish the hail size and storm severity at our specific address on the loss date.

Before we discuss any details, could you please let me know:

1. **Conflicts.** Whether you or your firm have any past or present relationship with **Fire Insurance Exchange / Farmers Insurance**, the **Kennedy Law Firm P.C.** (defense counsel), or **Prieve Engineering, LLC** (the insurer's engineer) that would prevent you from taking this engagement.
2. **Availability** to take on a new matter in this timeframe.
3. Your **CV**, professional **license(s)/certifications** (e.g., PE / CCM), and a **fee schedule**.
4. A **list of your prior expert testimony** (depositions/trials) and whether any court has ever excluded or limited your testimony.
5. Whether you are able to **inspect the property** in Eden Prairie and to review the opposing engineer's native photographs once produced.

I'm happy to provide the case documents and discuss scope once you've confirmed there's no conflict. Thank you for your time — I look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,
Yibiao Lu (and Jie Hu)
Plaintiffs pro se
[phone] | [email]

## Notes for you (do not send)

- **Send the same email to two or three candidates in parallel** (e.g., Christine Standohar-Alfano / ICC Forensics; American Engineering Testing; and one meteorologist — Bunkers or McGinnis). Compare responses on conflicts, fees, and testifying record.
- **Do not attach the file record to the first email.** Wait until the expert clears conflicts and you decide to move forward; then send the Complaint, the Answer, the Prieve report + Photo Appendix A, the appraisal award and demand, the NWS/ASOS weather data, and the scope tracker.
- If a firm asks you to sign an engagement letter or retainer, read the fee terms first and keep every invoice (your Consumer Fraud Act and § 604.18 claims allow recovery of certain costs/fees).
- Keep the tone the same for a meteorologist, but swap item 5 for: "whether you can determine the hail size, wind, and storm severity at our specific address on July 13–14, 2024 from radar/ASOS data."
