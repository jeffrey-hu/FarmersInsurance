## 4A. Proportionality (Rules 1 and 26.02)

*20-second version:*

> **We agree proportionality applies, and it favors Plaintiffs. Defendant alone holds these records. What remains is 15 yes-or-no admissions, 7 interrogatories and specific documents, after we withdrew 82 RFAs. Defendant hasn't identified any specific burden. And proportionality doesn't excuse a withholding statement, a privilege log, or Bates citations. Those are what three of our four items ask for.**

*If the Judge wants more:*
- *Procedural duties:* **Proportionality limits the scope of discovery. It does not excuse stating whether documents are withheld under Rule 34.02(c), logging what is withheld under Rule 26.02(f)(1), or citing Bates numbers when answering by reference to records under Rule 33.03.** *(Relief items 2, 3 and 4 rest on those duties.)*
- *Cost:* **Refusing yes-or-no admissions and pointing us to 2,340 pages costs more than answering. Defendant can cite its own Bates numbers in minutes; we would have to search everything. Rule 1 applies to Defendant too.**

*If the Judge wants to trim: offer the priority list in § 2 rather than leave the cuts to chance.*
