## 4. If Defendant says … → respond

| Defendant says | Response (short) |
|---|---|
| **"The claim was resolved by the binding award; nothing else is relevant."** | That is Defendant's Second Affirmative Defense, the disputed merits question. Discovery reaches "any party's claim or defense" (Rule 26.02(b)). Defendant's own appraiser's report records hail damage to another window, left out as pre-existing, and says the award was made "without consideration of any applicable coverage questions," with prior payments "not applied" (FIE 2317–2318). |
| **"Oppressive / not proportional."** | Defendant alone holds these records. Plaintiffs withdrew 82 of 141 RFAs; the 15 left are yes/no. Defendant has shown no specific burden. The demand was **$365,393.59**; the award was **$12,800** (FIE 2316–2317). |
| **"We agreed to answer 25 RFAs."** | Neither Rule 36 nor the Scheduling Order limits RFAs. Defendant set its own limit. Its stay request has not been heard, and on October 26 it served its own interrogatories, RFAs and RFPs on Plaintiffs. |
| **"Look in the 2,340 pages."** | Rule 33.03 allows that only if the answer specifies the records "in sufficient detail as to permit the interrogating party to locate and to identify" them. We ask for Bates numbers. |
| **"Interrogatory 7 asks for legal conclusions."** | Which jurisdiction Defendant says it lacks is a contention about its own pleading (Rule 33.02). Defendant should say which, or withdraw the allegation. One sworn line answers proper party. |
| **"Not ripe; we want to keep conferring."** | Defendant's November 5 letter: it will not answer more RFAs, has produced its "entire discoverable file," and we "will need to address [our] concerns with the court." It served no supplemental answer to any of the 15 RFAs or 7 interrogatories. |
| **"Discovery should wait for our summary-judgment motion."** | No stay has been entered. Paragraph 4 calls for this call before any motion. Defendant is taking discovery itself. |
| **"Interrogatory 2 is complete."** | Two parts remain: whether anything is withheld under its five objections, and if nothing is, withdrawal of the objections. |
| **"Plaintiffs sent too many conferral emails" / courtesy.** | Brief: the October 21 responses gave no date for the claim file; the November 5 production resolved that part. Do not argue about the hearing reschedule. |
| **Privilege log.** | Five pre-suit emails, December 2024–July 2025, logged as work product "regarding the claim investigation," with no authors or recipients. Rule 26.02(f)(1) requires enough description to assess the claim. |
