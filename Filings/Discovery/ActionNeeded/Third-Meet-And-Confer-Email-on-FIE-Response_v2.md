**1. The 2024 inspections and the September 3 and November 4 letters** (Complaint ¶¶ 53, 69(a))

Hancock Claims Consultants inspected the Property on August 29, 2024 and delivered its report to Defendant on August 30, 2024 (FIE 0977). Defendant's answer to Complaint ¶ 14 denies that Plaintiffs cooperated with the claim-handling process "to the extent Plaintiffs attempted to direct the claim-handling process by adamantly advising Hancock Claims Consultants that the only thing that needed to be inspected was the windows on the inside where they were leaking." Defendant's September 3, 2024 letter states: "As part of our investigation we had your home inspected. At the inspection, interior water damage around your windows was observed. There were no other damages observed" (FIE 0051).

Mr. Brau inspected the Property on September 6, 2024, after that letter issued (FIE 1014). On September 10, 2024, he wrote that he had "discussed your claim and photos with my MN field supervisor and we will be having a structural engineer contact you" (FIE 1002). Plaintiffs asked for Mr. Brau's inspection notes on October 7 and October 28, 2025 (FIE 1736, 1754). Defendant replied that "[t]he file does not contain a report on the requested date" (FIE 1660) and that "[a]ll the information we can release has already been sent to you" (FIE 1757).

*The requests and Defendant's responses:*
- **RFP No. 51** (documents on Hancock's photograph captions, such as "Window Wrap North Damaged - No"). The response is objection only, and nothing responsive has been produced.
