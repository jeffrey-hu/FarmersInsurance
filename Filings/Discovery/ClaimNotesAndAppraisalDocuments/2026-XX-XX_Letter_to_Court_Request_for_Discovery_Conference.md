Dear Judge Anderson:

Under ¶ 4 of the Court's September 8, 2026 Scheduling Order, Plaintiffs respectfully request a conference call regarding Defendant's responses to two categories of Plaintiffs' Requests for Production: (1) the claim file and claim notes, and (2) appraisal materials. Plaintiffs have limited this request to those two categories.

**Conferral.** Plaintiffs served their Requests for Production on September 15, 2026. Defendant responded on October 21, 2026. On [date], Plaintiffs emailed defense counsel identifying the specific deficiencies below and requesting production by November 6, 2026, and stated that Plaintiffs would request a conference call with the Court if the issues could not be resolved. [Describe Defendant's response accurately, e.g.: "Defendant responded on [date] that ___." / "Defendant did not respond." / "Defendant produced ___ on [date] but did not produce ___."]

**1. Claim file and claim notes (RFP Nos. 1 and 20).** RFP No. 1 requests the entire claim file, "including every … system or case note, audit-log entry … and internal communication of any kind." RFP No. 20 requests the claims-portal record. Defendant's response to RFP No. 1 states that "[d]ue to technical difficulties, Defendant will supplement its document production with the redacted Full Claim file/Claim notes as soon as possible." Defendant's Initial Disclosures state that it "has in its possession the remainder of the claim file not produced herewith." As of the date of this letter, no claim notes, activity log or portal record has been produced, and no production date has been given. [Update if anything has changed.]

**2. Appraisal materials (RFP Nos. 15, 16 and 17; Concise communications under RFP No. 1).** RFP Nos. 15–17 request Defendant's correspondence with the appraisal panel, any part of the umpire's working file in Defendant's possession, and the file and report of Defendant's appraiser, Greg Tomes.

Defendant's own production shows that responsive documents exist:
- The appraiser's email that Defendant forwarded to Plaintiffs as "the appraiser's response" states, "I mistakenly stated the dining room in my report" (FIE 1639; FIE 1645). That report has not been produced.
- Defendant addressed its August 13, 2025 appraisal letter to Concise Resolution Services, LLC together with Plaintiffs' appraiser (FIE 0308). On September 15, 2025, Concise emailed Defendant's claim mailbox that "[t]his appraisal is complete and the closing docs are attached" (FIE 1633). The attachments have not been produced.

RFP No. 16 also asked Defendant, if it has no report, estimate, worksheet, calculation or methodology document from the umpire, to "state that expressly." Defendant's response does not do so. Defendant's responses to RFP Nos. 1, 15, 16, 17 and 20 do not state whether any responsive materials are being withheld, as Minn. R. Civ. P. 34.02(c) requires, and no description of withheld privileged material has been provided, as Rule 26.02(f)(1) requires.

**Relevance and proportionality.** Defendant objects to nearly every request on the ground that the claim "was resolved via a binding appraisal award." Plaintiffs contend that the award reaches only what the panel actually evaluated (Count III). Defendant contends that it resolved the entire claim (Second Affirmative Defense). What the panel was asked to decide and what it evaluated is therefore the central disputed question in this case. Documents showing what the panel did are "relevant to any party's claim or defense" under Rule 26.02(b). Defendant's Initial Disclosures themselves identify the appraisal award and all three panel members as documents and witnesses Defendant relies on. The documents requested here are specific and identified, and the claim file is something Defendant has already undertaken to produce.

**Plaintiffs' efforts to narrow discovery.** Plaintiffs have tried to keep discovery proportional:
- On September 27, 2026, in response to Defendant's proposal to answer only 25 of 141 Requests for Admission, Plaintiffs withdrew 82 requests, asked Defendant to answer 37, and offered to withdraw 22 more if Defendant admitted five requests concerning its status as the proper party.
- On October 9, 2026, Defendant rejected that proposal without a counter-proposal.

Plaintiffs have not brought the Requests for Admission dispute in this letter and reserve it, along with Defendant's other objections and incomplete responses, for separate resolution.

**Relief requested.** Plaintiffs request a conference call and, if the issues are not resolved, an order requiring Defendant, within 14 days:
1. to produce the complete claim file, including claim/system notes, the activity log and the claims-portal record (RFP Nos. 1 and 20);
2. to produce all documents responsive to RFP Nos. 15–17, including the report referenced at FIE 1639, and all communications with Concise Resolution Services, LLC concerning the Claim or the appraisal, including the attachments to its September 15, 2025 email;
3. to state expressly, under RFP No. 16, whether it has any report, estimate, worksheet, calculation or methodology document from the umpire; and
4. to state for each of these requests whether any materials are being withheld (Rule 34.02(c)), and to describe anything withheld as privileged (Rule 26.02(f)(1)).

Plaintiffs are available for a call at the Court's convenience.
