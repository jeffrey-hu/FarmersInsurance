## Timing note

The letter was transmitted after 5:00 p.m. That has **no** effect here. Minn. R. Civ. P.
6.01(e)'s one-day addition applies to periods that run *from* service; these are fixed
calendar dates stated in the letter itself. The rule will matter for the discovery sets,
which are service — sent after 5:00 p.m., the 30-day response period gains a day.

## Why it matters

1. **Minn. Gen. R. Prac. 115.10** requires a conferral before any motion. This letter,
   plus whatever Defendant does or does not say by September 29 and October 9, is that
   record for every scheduling or discovery motion that follows.
2. **Item 5 is the clock.** Minn. R. Civ. P. 21 permits parties to be added only by court
   order, and Gen. R. Prac. 115.04 requires a motion served and filed 21 days before its
   hearing, on a hearing date first obtained under Rule 115.02. A joinder motion must
   therefore be on file in **late October 2026**, not on the November 16, 2026 deadline.
3. **Silence is also an answer.** If Defendant does not respond by October 9, the letter
   is the good-cause showing a Minn. Gen. R. Prac. 111.04 motion to extend the joinder
   deadline will need — and that motion must itself be *made before* November 16.
