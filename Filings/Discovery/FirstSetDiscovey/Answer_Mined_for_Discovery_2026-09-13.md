## 2. Affirmative denials that create interrogable contentions

A denial of an allegation puts Plaintiffs to their proof and commits FIE to nothing. An **affirmative statement** in an Answer is different — FIE must be able to support it. These are the affirmative ones.

### 2.1 ¶33 — the itemization denial ⚠️ *highest value in the Answer*

Complaint ¶33 alleged the award "does not state actual value and loss separately to each item," identifies the windows only by "F1.7" and "B1.2," and states a single lump-sum $12,800 RCV.

> **ANSWER:** "FIE **denies** that the Appraisal Award does not state actual value and loss separately to each item. The Appraisal Award speaks for itself."

FIE has taken the affirmative position that the award **is** itemized within the meaning of Minn. Stat. § 65A.01, subd. 3. That position is testable against a one-page document Plaintiffs already hold.

**What is already in the sets:** **RFA 9** asks FIE to admit the award "states a single lump-sum Replacement Cost Value of $12,800 and does not separately state the actual cash value and loss for each awarded window." Given ¶33, FIE will deny it.

**What is missing:** nothing makes FIE *identify where* the per-item values appear. A denial without a citation is an assertion; a sworn answer that cannot point to a line on the award is the Count III argument made for you.

**Recommended — 1 interrogatory.** This is the one place in this review I would spend from the budget.

> Identify each portion of The Appraisal Award that You contend states the actual value and the loss separately for each item, specifying for each awarded item the actual value and the loss You contend the award states and the location on the award where each appears; and if You contend the award states a value or loss for any item other than the two awarded windows, identify that item and the amount.

**Counts as:** 1 — particulars about a single contention.

### 2.2 ¶40 — the flat denial of the October 28, 2025 leak report

Complaint ¶40 alleged Plaintiffs reported active window leaks on October 28, 2025 and that FIE did not respond on the merits.

> **ANSWER:** "FIE **denies** paragraph 40."

Not "without knowledge" — a flat denial of a dated communication. Either FIE has no record of it, which is itself worth establishing, or it has one and the denial is unsupportable.

**Recommended — documentary.** Add to the RFP set:

> All documents concerning any report by Plaintiffs of water intrusion, leaking, or active damage at The Property on or about October 28, 2025, including the communication itself, every claim-system entry recording its receipt, and every internal or external communication concerning it. If no such document exists, state so expressly.

*(Note: **RFP 41** already asks for documents concerning water intrusion "from July 13, 2024 through the present," which likely reaches the underlying communication. The value added here is the express "if none exists, state so expressly," which converts a silent production into an answer.)*

### 2.3 ¶42 — the targeted scope-description denial

> **ANSWER:** "FIE denies paragraph 42 **to the extent that it alleges 'an incompleteness in Defendant's scope description that extends even to a window the appraisal panel evaluated and awarded.'**"

FIE denied one clause with surgical precision while pleading no knowledge of the rest — which means someone read it closely and decided that clause mattered. It concerns the garage-elevation window that appears nowhere in FIE's inspection record (¶38). **Interrogatory 1 and RFP 19** reach the surrounding ground. No new item recommended; flagged because a precision denial marks the spot FIE is most worried about.

## 3. Documents FIE's own Answer names, which nothing requests by name

### 3.1 Greg Tomes's report ⚠️

¶36's answer quotes FIE's own appraiser: *"The damage to the windows on the rear elevation was in the family room. I mistakenly stated the dining room **in my report**."* FIE also admits it "forwarded to Plaintiff Lu the **appraiser's position** on the windows" on September 18, 2025, and sent a September 19, 2025 letter "**attaching the appraiser's response**."

So there exist, on FIE's own pleading: **a report by Greg Tomes**, an "appraiser's position" document, and an "appraiser's response" attachment.

**RFP 17** asks broadly for Tomes's file "including his notes, photographs, measurements, methodology, drafts, invoices, and billing records." It does not name a *report*, and FIE has been careful elsewhere — ¶34 admits only that **the umpire** "did not provide **FIE** with a report or estimate."

**Recommended:** amend RFP 17 to add, or add a new request for:

> The report of Greg Tomes referred to in Your answer to paragraph 36 of the Complaint, together with the "appraiser's position" forwarded to Plaintiff Lu on September 18, 2025, the "appraiser's response" attached to Your September 19, 2025 letter, and every draft of each.

### 3.2 The "September 4, 2024 claim outcome correspondence" ⚠️

¶53's answer denies that FIE's "**September 4, 2024** claim outcome correspondence was improper in any way." The first denial is dated **September 3, 2024**. No September 4 document appears in the record or in `ClaimTimeline.md`.

**Recommended:**

> All claim outcome correspondence, letters, or claim-system entries dated September 4, 2024 concerning The Claim. If no such document exists, state so expressly and identify the document You intended to refer to in Your answer to paragraph 53 of the Complaint.

**Either answer is useful.** A document produces something nobody has seen. "No such document exists" establishes that FIE's verified pleading misdescribes its own denial letter — which bears on the care taken with the rest of it.

### 3.3 The Hancock "Damaged - No" tagging

¶15 alleged Hancock produced a photo set "of every window wrap, each tagged **'Damaged-No.'**" ¶53 alleged Hancock "uniformly marked every inspected component of the home — windows, siding, garage, and downspouts — as 'Damaged - No'."

FIE's ¶15 answer admits only that Hancock inspected on August 29, 2024 and that "The Hancock Claims Consultants **Inspection Photos** speak for themselves." **The tagging is neither admitted nor denied**, in either paragraph.

**RFP 4** asks for Hancock's work "including the narrative report, all field notes, measurement sheets, **inspection-application data**, and the complete photograph set" — which should capture the tagging fields. **No new request needed.** But the tagging deserves a request for admission, because a uniform "Damaged - No" across components that Prieve later photographed as dented is the cleanest single fact in the case.

**Recommended RFA:**

> Admit that the Hancock Claims Consultants inspection record for the August 29, 2024 inspection marks each inspected component of The Property "Damaged - No."

## 4. The Jie Hu gap ⚠️

| Paragraph | What Plaintiffs pleaded | What FIE answered |
|---|---|---|
| **¶2** | "Policy … issued by Defendant Fire Insurance Exchange" | "FIE admits that it issued a Next Generation Homeowners Policy … **to Plaintiff Yibiao Lu**" |
| **¶4** | Jie Hu "is an insured under the policy, which defines 'you' and 'your' to mean the named insured … **and the named insured's spouse if a resident of the same household**" | Admits Yibiao Lu resides there, is the named insured, and the property is owner-occupied. **Says nothing about Jie Hu.** |
| **¶9** | "Plaintiff Yibiao Lu and Defendant entered into Policy…" | "Policy ending in 49-65, **issued to Plaintiff Yibiao Lu**, was effective 10/22/2023 through 10/22/2024" |

Three chances to acknowledge the second plaintiff's status; none taken. Under Rule 8.04 an averment not denied is admitted — but FIE's ¶4 answer is a partial admission that pointedly omits Jie Hu, which is not the same as silence, and FIE has reserved "lack of jurisdiction" and "failure to state a claim" in Affirmative Defense 1.

**Recommended — Requests for Admission (free), not an interrogatory:**

> Admit that Jie Hu is an insured under The Policy.
>
> Admit that The Policy defines "you" and "your" to include the named insured's spouse if a resident of the same household.
>
> Admit that You do not contend Jie Hu lacks standing to assert a claim under The Policy.

The third is the one that matters. Rule 36.01 does not permit "we reserve the right to contend otherwise."

## 5. The policy-form problem, now sharper

FIE's answers to ¶¶2, 9 and 10 admit **only** that the policy was "effective 10/22/2023 through 10/22/2024," and plead no knowledge of:

- continuous renewal since October 22, 2014 (¶9);
- the Coverage A limit of $886,000, the 2% windstorm/hail deductible of $17,720, the enumerated hail peril, endorsements J6898, J6944, MN079 and MN080, and the two-year suit-limitation provision (**¶10**).

FIE pleaded that it is "without sufficient information or knowledge" about **the terms of its own policy**, adding only "The policy speaks for itself." It then relied on the $17,720 deductible in ¶¶2 and 35 to justify paying nothing.

**Consequence for discovery:** the operative **form** — not the declarations — is the document the case turns on, and the project holds only the 2023 renewal declarations. Every loss-settlement argument rests on page 24 of 37 of the **2014** form.

**No new request needed. RFP 35** (the complete policy as delivered at the October 22, 2023 renewal) and **RFP 24** (the form and endorsements applicable on the loss date) already reach it. **Treat both as first-priority**, and note that FIE's ¶10 answer forecloses any later argument that the policy terms were never in issue.

## 6. Pleading-level evasions worth a meet-and-confer note

FIE pleaded "without sufficient information or knowledge" as to matters **within its own records** at ¶¶10, 13, 16 (remainder), 17 (remainder), 23(a)–(e), and 41. Minn. R. Civ. P. 8.02 permits that plea only where the party "is without knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief," and it operates as a denial. For its own claim file, its own policy terms and its own 2020 scope, the plea is difficult to sustain.

**This is not a discovery request.** It is material for the Scheduling Order ¶4 letter if FIE's discovery responses repeat the pattern — and it is why **Instruction 9** (partial objections must state what is withheld) and **Rule 33.01(d)**'s duty on the signing officer to "furnish such information as is available" were added to the interrogatory set.
