# 4. Requests for Admission

Rule 36 has **no numeric limit**, and Rule 36.02 makes an admission **conclusively established for the action** — stronger than an interrogatory answer (evidence) or a production (authentication still required). That is why most of what follows lands here.

> **Numbering.** The served set runs 1–34 and the pending A-items propose 35–76. Number these **after** the A-item decisions are made. If all A-items are adopted, AM-8 through AM-15 run **[77]–[94]**, and AM-16 takes it to [96].

## AM-10 ⚠️ — The $0 siding and debris lines · 4 requests · *highest-value item in either pass*

**What FIE did.** Complaint ¶31 pleaded, in detail, that the award records "Hail" in the form's "Loss Cause" field **and in a per-item "Causation" column**, and that the panel listed a **"Siding & Paint"** line and a **"Debris"** line — *each likewise marked "Hail"* — at **$0 RCV and $0 ACV**.

FIE's answer admits the three appraisers' identities and that Roos and Foster signed. **On the causation column, the siding line, the debris line and the $0 values it says only: "The Appraisal Award speaks for itself."** Not admitted, not denied.

**Why this is the biggest miss.** This is the strongest structural fact in the case, and the project knows it. From `OffenseStrategies/AppraisalAwardStrategy/00_Overview.md`: a $0 award on an item the panel **expressly found hail-caused** is by definition an *amount or coverage* determination, not a causation finding. Under *Quade* the hail finding binds FIE on the siding; the $0 is a coverage call the Court decides de novo. It is pleaded at Complaint ¶31, ¶50, ¶57(j) and ¶61.

**And the word *siding* does not appear once in the 34 served-ready RFAs, or in any of the 42 pending A-item requests.** RFA 4 covers only *"hail as the cause of loss for the two windows it awarded."* Exhibit C (the award) is already attached and authenticated by RFA 34 — so these are free documentary admissions against a document FIE itself calls binding.

> Admit that The Appraisal Award contains a column captioned "Causation."
>
> Admit that The Appraisal Award marks the "Siding & Paint" line item "Hail" in the Causation column and states a Replacement Cost Value of $0 and an Actual Cash Value of $0 for that line item.
>
> Admit that The Appraisal Award marks the "Debris" line item "Hail" in the Causation column and states a Replacement Cost Value of $0 and an Actual Cash Value of $0 for that line item.
>
> Admit that The Appraisal Award does not state the actual value and the loss separately for siding repaint and for siding replace-and-remove, the two siding items identified in Plaintiffs' August 3, 2025 Demand for Appraisal.

An admission on the second converts Complaint ¶57(j) and ¶61 from allegation to established fact, from FIE's own binding award. The fourth is the Count III itemization argument applied to the siding, which **AM-1** does not reach because AM-1 is directed at the windows.

⚠️ **Quote the award's captions exactly as they appear on Exhibit C before serving.** A caption mismatch is how RFA 18 became deniable — see §7.

## 6.1 Rule 8.02 — "without sufficient information" as to FIE's own records

FIE pleaded "without sufficient information or knowledge" as to matters **within its own records** at **¶¶10, 13, 16 (remainder), 17 (remainder), 23(a)–(e), 38, and 41** — its own policy terms, its own claim file, its own 2020 scope, and whether its file records a window on the insured dwelling. **Affirmative Defense 3** adds a general catch-all denial on top.
