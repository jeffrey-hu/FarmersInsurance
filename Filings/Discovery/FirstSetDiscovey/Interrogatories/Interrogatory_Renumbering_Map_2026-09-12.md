## Why this document exists

Between September 11 and 12, 2026, five interrogatories were removed from the First Set and one was deferred to a second set. That left the document numbered **2–6, 9–13, 17–20** — gaps at 1, 7, 8, 14, 15 and 16 — which cannot be served.

Renumbering was deliberately deferred until every removal decision was final, rather than done after each one, because roughly **130 references to specific interrogatory numbers** exist across nine project files and each pass would have invalidated all of them. This is the single renumbering pass. It was safe to run once **I-28 was decided on Option A** (rewrite in place rather than merge), which was the last open item capable of changing the numbering. **I-24 remains open but is a trim of Interrogatory 11's lettered subparts, not a removal, and does not affect numbering.**

## How to read the other project files
