## 2. Duplication

### 2.1 One real overlap, created yesterday

**Interrogatory 1** (as rewritten under I-28 Option A) closes with: *"…if You contend The Appraisal Award determined the question for that window, identify the portion of the award You rely on."*

**Interrogatory 10** asks: *"State each fact on which You base any contention that The Appraisal Award resolved The Claim … and identify every window that the award is intended to cover and every window it is intended to exclude…"*

These reach the same ground from two directions. No. 10 asks globally which windows the award covers; No. 1's closing clause asks, per window, whether FIE says the award decided it. FIE can answer No. 1's clause with "see response to Interrogatory 10" and be substantially right.

This was not a problem on September 11 — the award clause was added to Interrogatory 1 as part of I-28, hours before the renumbering. See **Q-3** for two ways to resolve it. It is the only duplication in the set that is not deliberate.

RFP 22 asks for "all prior-claim inspection records for The Property, including the inspection records for the 2014, 2015, and 2020 claims," which reaches the 2014 and 2015 items by implication. **It does not reach the 2021 worksheet or the 2022 letter and estimate at all**, and those are the two that should worry you: they fall between The 2020 Claim and The Claim, and nothing in the pleadings or the discovery explains what they are. A Farmers letter *and estimate* dated February 18, 2022 implies claim activity — or a valuation — in a period the case does not currently account for.

The weather records matter for a different reason: Prieve concluded that storm data does not document a July 13, 2024 event, a conclusion the family's NWS material contradicts. Which records Prieve consulted is the first question in dismantling that conclusion, and no request names them.

**See Q-9.** The cheapest fix is documentary — these belong in the RFP set or the second set — with at most one interrogatory.

### 3.2 The September 3, 2024 denial's factual premise is not interrogated

Interrogatory 2 is aimed at the **November 4** letter's exclusions. Interrogatory 9 asks about supervisory review of both letters. Nothing asks FIE to state the basis for the September 3 letter's affirmative assertion that **"There were no other damages observed."**

That assertion is the foundation of the shifting-rationale argument: the September 3 letter says nothing was damaged; the November 4 letter concedes damage and blames wear and tear. The two are incompatible, and § 72A.201 subd. 5 makes inconsistent claim-handling practice actionable. **See Q-10.**

### 3.3 Deliberately absent — confirmed, not overlooked

Reserves, claim-handler training, and expert identification are all out by earlier decision, not oversight. Endorsement J6944 is held in the second-set draft pending FIE actually asserting it. No change recommended.

## 4. Holes an adversary could use

| # | Hole | Exposure |
|---|---|---|
| **H1** | "**the six** policy exclusions" (Interrogatory 2) | The attachment cites A.4, A.7, A.8, A.9, A.14 and B.4, B.5, B.6, B.8, B.9 — ten numbered provisions, several duplicated across the two sections. Six is a count of *unique concepts*, not of what the letter cites. FIE can answer that the letter cites no such thing. **Q-1** |
| **H2** | All ten called "exclusions" | Section I.**A** is titled "Uninsured Types of Loss or Damage"; Section I.**B** is "Excluded Causes of Loss or Damage." Under *Travelers Indem. Co. v. Bloomington Steel*, 718 N.W.2d 888, the insurer must establish an **exclusion**. If FIE recharacterises marring or wear-and-tear as a limit on the grant of coverage, the burden flips to Plaintiffs — and nothing in the set makes FIE commit either way. **Q-1** |
| **H3** ✅ *fixed 9/12 in all three sets* | "the Prieve Report's Photo Appendix A" was undefined | "The Prieve Report" is defined as the report "together with all drafts thereof." Photo Appendix A was transmitted as a **separate 110-page PDF** titled "PHOTO APPENDIX A." Interrogatories 1 and 3 both depend on it. The narrative does cite photographs by A-number, so the link is defensible — but a definition removes the argument entirely. **Q-4** |
| **H4** ✅ *fixed 9/12, all three sets* | Policy number format | The definitions say **98055-49-65**; the denial letter says **0980554965**. Same digits, different presentation and a leading zero. Harmless until someone wants it to be harmful. **Q-5** |
| **H5** ✅ *fixed 9/12 — now thirty (30)* | Certification ceiling of "fewer than forty (40)" | The careful count is **27**, and 25 if I-24 is applied. Certifying a ceiling of 39 volunteers a number thirteen higher than the truth, and concedes room Plaintiffs may later want back. **Q-6** |
| **H6** | Interrogatory 2's mismatched series | "…state X, and identify Y; and identify Z." A comma-joined pair followed by a semicolon-joined third reads as a drafting slip, and in a document FIE will quote, slips invite argument about what the question asks. **Q-2** |
| **H7** ✅ *fixed 9/12* | Instruction 6 and Interrogatory 14 used different language for the same duty | Instruction 6: "identify it, describe its disposition, and identify each person with knowledge." Interrogatory 14 asks for type, date, author, subject matter, date and manner of destruction, who authorised it, the retention schedule, and custodians. FIE can answer the narrower instruction and call the interrogatory satisfied. **Q-11** |

## 5. Conciseness

Only two places are worth touching, and one of them is already queued.

**Interrogatory 11** — 4 lettered subparts, the longest in the set. **I-24 is still open** and would cut (b) and (c), taking it to two subparts and the count from 4 to 2. That is the conciseness fix; nothing further is needed. *(See Q-15.)*

**Interrogatory 12** — six lettered subparts plus a trailing demand for the attorney-in-fact instrument. It is the most expensive interrogatory in the set at **+6**, and it reads as a list because it is one. **I would not shorten it.** The November 16, 2026 joinder deadline is the reason it exists, each subpart names a different entity-identification question, and compressing them invites a compound answer that identifies nothing. Recorded here so the length is understood as deliberate. *(Q-8 sets out a two-subpart saving if the budget ever needs it.)*

**Interrogatory 5's closing sentence** is an assertion, not a question — *"The correspondence between a given line item and a specific window or sash is not ascertainable from the estimate itself…"*. That is deliberate (I-30): it puts Rule 33.03's "substantially the same burden" condition in issue on the face of the interrogatory. It will read oddly to anyone who does not know why it is there. No change; noted so it is not "cleaned up" later.

Everything else is already at or near minimum length for what it asks.

## 6. Smaller points

- ✅ *Fixed September 12, 2026.* **Interrogatory 6** asked for "date, speaker, **audience**, and medium." "Audience" is unusual for a two-party telephone call and a voicemail; **"recipient(s)"** is the ordinary term and matches the Identify definition. **Q-7**
- ❌ *Raised and rejected September 12, 2026 — the preamble stays as is; see Q-12.* **The preamble** says answers are due "within thirty (30) days after service." Service by U.S. Mail adds three days under Rule 6.01(e). The preamble is not wrong — 30 days is the rule — but the certificate of service says the set goes by mail, so the two read inconsistently to a lay eye. **Q-12**
- **"The Policy"** is defined as the policy "in force on July 13, 2024," which is correct, but the operative **form** is whatever was delivered at the October 22, 2023 renewal and the project holds only the renewal declarations. RFP 35 and RFA item A-7 are the routes to closing that. No change to this document; flagged so the dependency is visible. **Q-13**
- **Interrogatory 13** applies the full "Identify" definition — name, title, employer, business address, telephone, email — to *every person with knowledge of any fact at issue*. That is a large ask and the most likely target for a proportionality objection in the set. **Q-14** offers a narrowing that keeps the substance.
