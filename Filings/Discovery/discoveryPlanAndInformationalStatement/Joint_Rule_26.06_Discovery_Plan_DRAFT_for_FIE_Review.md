**Defendant's position on the schedule.** *[Defendant to supply.]*

**Plaintiffs' position on the schedule.** This is a document-intensive first-party insurance case: a multi-year claim file spanning two claims, three separate inspections by two outside firms, non-party discovery from those firms and from the members of the appraisal panel, and competing expert causation testimony. Certain information Plaintiffs sought during the claim process — the damage locations from the 2020 claim, the records of the September 6, 2024 inspection, and the identification of the windows covered by the September 12, 2025 award — was not provided or was described inconsistently, and resolving those questions will require written discovery, a documented custodial search, and an organizational deposition under Rule 30.02(f) that cannot be scheduled until that search is complete. Plaintiffs further propose (a) a date certain for substantial completion of document production, so that depositions and non-party discovery may be scheduled against a settled record; (b) a deadline to join parties or amend pleadings falling after that date, because Defendant's first affirmative defense contests its identity as the proper party and the documents bearing on that question are within Defendant's production; (c) a separate, later deadline for a motion under Minn. Stat. § 604.18, which by statute may not be pleaded in the original complaint and requires a prima facie showing drawn from the discovery record; and (d) that dispositive motions be heard after the close of expert discovery, so that neither party must brief summary judgment on an incomplete expert record.
