## Why this interrogatory is held

**FIE has not invoked J6944.** The November 4, 2024 denial rested on six other exclusions — wear and tear, marring, corrosion/deterioration/rust, inherent vice or latent defect, and neglect or lack of maintenance. Endorsement J6944 (Limited Matching of Undamaged Property) is a matching argument **Plaintiffs** raised, at Complaint ¶51, in anticipation of a defense FIE has not yet made.

An interrogatory premised on a contention the other side has not made invites a one-line answer — "Defendant makes no such contention at this time" — that costs a slot from a capped budget and tells you nothing. Worse, it signposts the argument and gives FIE months to prepare it.

Served second, after FIE has actually taken a matching position, the same question is aimed at a live contention and FIE cannot dispose of it that way.

## Trigger conditions — serve only after (1), plus at least one of (2) and (3)

1. **FIE actually asserts J6944 or a matching limitation** — in an amended answer, a discovery response, a denial of one of the replacement-cost Requests for Admission (RFA item **A-10**, Requests 61–65), a mediation position paper, or summary-judgment briefing. *This condition is mandatory. Without it there is no contention to interrogate.*
2. **Request 24** (First Set) is responded to: the policy form and endorsements J6898, **J6944**, MN079 and MN080 applicable on the loss date, with drafting history and any underwriting or explanatory bulletins concerning J6944.
3. **Request 40** (First Set) is responded to: FIE's interpretation, guidance, training, or claims-handling practice concerning J6944 and the matching obligation addressed in *Cedar Bluff Townhome Condominium Ass'n v. American Family Mut. Ins. Co.*, 857 N.W.2d 290 (Minn. 2014).

**What to look for.** If FIE's own guidance documents read J6944 more narrowly than the position it takes in this case, the interrogatory becomes a request to reconcile the two under oath. If FIE never asserts matching at all, **do not serve this** — the slot is worth more elsewhere, and Complaint ¶51 is then unopposed on the point.

**A related check worth making at the same time.** RFP 35 seeks the complete policy as delivered at the October 22, 2023 renewal. If FIE cannot show it delivered J6944 at that renewal, the endorsement may not be available to it at all — which would make this interrogatory unnecessary rather than merely premature.
