## 1. The selection rules

1. **Aim at FIE's summary-judgment motion.** FIE's 12/9 memorandum will rest on its Second Affirmative Defense: the award is binding, the panel looked at every window, and anything outside the award is prior-claim or other-storm damage. Every request below either takes away one of those premises or makes FIE commit to it under oath.
2. **Build on the 11/5 production.** Most of these requests rest on documents FIE first produced on 11/5 (Tomes closing report, photo sheet, claim notes, Brau → Prieve email). That answers "duplicative" and "oppressive": they are follow-up on FIE's own new documents.
3. **Nothing that is before the Court on 11/19.** The Declaration of Appraisers, the 9/4/2025 umpire transmittal, the 2020 claim file, the proper-party RFAs, Brau's notes, Interrogatories 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 10, 11 and the privilege log are all in the ¶ 4 relief. Re-asking them now would let FIE say we are serving around the conference.
4. **No RFA that asks what a document says.** FIE answered every document-text RFA with "speaks for itself" / "bound by the Appraisal Award" (RFAs 14–21, 23–25). The RFAs below ask for genuineness, FIE's contentions, or facts about FIE's own records, which that answer does not meet.

## 3. Interrogatories (5)

**Rule 33.01(a) check:** the First Set served 12 numbered interrogatories. These five add about 9 on a subpart count. Recount the First Set's subparts before service and state the cumulative figure.

**INT 1 (the "all windows" contention).** State whether You contend that the appraisal panel inspected every window at The Property and determined that no window other than those designated "F1.7" and "B1.2" sustained hail damage on July 13, 2024. If You do, state each fact supporting that contention and identify each person with knowledge of it and each document recording it. If You do not, identify each window You contend the panel inspected and determined.
- **Why:** this is FIE's main SJ argument (its Interrogatory 5 answer: the appraisers "identified the two windows they determined were damaged from hail"; the 11/5 letter: "all of the windows"). Held from the First Set on purpose (Decision 7; September Draft Int 2). FIE cannot call it premature while moving for summary judgment on it.
- **Caution (*Rymer*):** panel testimony cannot be used to explain or alter the award. FIE's answer is still its contention, which is what we need to see.

**INT 2 (which windows are F1.7 and B1.2).** Identify, by elevation, room and sash, each window unit You contend The Appraisal Award designates "F1.7" and "B1.2"; state the basis for that identification; and state whether You contend The Appraisal Award includes "the three windows in the family room on the rear elevation" described at FIE 2317.
- **Why:** FIE's own adjuster "could not assume" what the notations mean (FIE 2293), and Tomes describes four windows where counsel says "two window units." FIE served the same question on us (its Interrogatory 12), so it cannot call it improper.

**INT 3 (other storms / pre-existing damage).** If You contend that damage to any window at The Property, other than the windows designated "F1.7" and "B1.2," was caused by an event other than the July 13, 2024 storm, or existed before July 13, 2024, identify each such window and sash, the event and date You contend caused the damage, and each fact and document supporting that contention.
- **Why:** this is FIE's fallback (Tomes declined to sign over "the date of loss," FIE 2318). It forces FIE to choose between its engineer ("most recent hail … 2020"; no recent hail damage) and other storms (SJ Notes 1). Either answer helps.
- **Caution:** FIE's own Benchmark report lists hail near the address in 2021–2023 (FIE 0903–0904). FIE will likely cite it. That is FIE's argument whether we ask or not; asking pins it down before depositions. Nothing in our own answers should concede any later storm damaged this home (SJ Notes 1, caution 4).

**INT 4 (what the umpire received, and from whom).** Identify each communication before September 12, 2025 between umpire Wade Roos (or Jade Claims LLC) and You, Concise Resolution Services, LLC, or Gregory Tomes, stating for each its date, sender, recipients (including whether John Foster received it), and the documents transmitted, including the "coverage letter, engineering report, demand estimate, weather report, prior claim report" recorded at FIE 2338.
- **Why:** the umpire received FIE's denial letter, the Prieve report and a "prior claim report" before the inspection. If Foster was not copied, that is a Count III misconduct fact; either way it shows the panel was given coverage material. The ¶ 4 relief asks only for the 9/4 transmittal; this reaches recipients and any calls.
- **Expect:** "Concise is not FIE." Answer: Tomes was FIE's appointed appraiser, Concise billed FIE, and Rule 33.01 requires information available to the party, including through its agents.

**INT 5 (people for depositions).** Identify by name, employer, title and last known contact information: (a) the "supervisor" with whom Plaintiffs' January 31 and February 13, 2025 submissions were discussed (FIE 2290–2291); (b) "Jayden" of Hancock Claims Consultants (FIE 2286); and (c) each source of the statement recorded on May 29, 2025 that Plaintiffs "had shown up at his home address" (FIE 2290–2291).
- **Why:** (a) and (b) are needed to notice depositions before 3/15/2027. (c) answers FIE's Interrogatory 13 theme: Roberts's note gives no source, and Prieve did not write it.
- **Alternate:** the FIE 1089 agency question ("whether any act in handling The Claim was performed by or for an entity other than Fire Insurance Exchange"), which FIE said on 11/5 "was not included in your discovery." Use it instead of (c) only if the proper-party trade fails on 11/19.
