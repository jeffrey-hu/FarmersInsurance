## 1. The Theory of the Case in One Paragraph

The case Plaintiffs will tell the jury and the court is straightforward: **the Lu/Hu family bought ten years of windstorm-and-hail coverage on a primary residence; on July 13, 2024 the home took a documented hailstorm; Farmers' own subsequently-procured appraisal panel confirmed hail caused the loss; and Farmers nonetheless paid $0 by stacking exclusions whose factual premises its own panel rejected, by imposing an "engineer's report only" evidentiary gate found nowhere in the policy, by losing or never creating its own adjuster's contemporaneous inspection record, and by producing a binding award so opaque that Farmers itself cannot describe the scope consistently.** That narrative is on every page of the project's primary record. The litigation strategy is to convert the existing record into a clean liability picture and force Farmers to defend conduct it cannot defend.

## 3. What Plaintiffs Can Force Farmers to Prove — And How to Neutralize Each

This is the underrated half of the playbook. Several Farmers positions are *defenses* on which Defendant bears the burden of proof. Plaintiffs' strategy is to pin Defendant down on each, then collapse the defense by attacking its factual or legal premise.

### 3.1 Each Section I exclusion Farmers asserts — Defendant's burden

Under Minnesota law, "the insured has the burden of proving coverage, but the insurer has the burden of proving any exclusion." *Henning Nelson Constr. Co. v. Fireman's Fund Am. Life Ins. Co.*, 383 N.W.2d 645, 651–52 (Minn. 1986); *SCSC Corp. v. Allied Mut. Ins. Co.*, 536 N.W.2d 305, 314 (Minn. 1995). Farmers' 11/4/2024 denial cited six exclusions:

| Exclusion | Burden | How Plaintiffs Defeat It |
|---|---|---|
| Wear and Tear (§ I.A.7 / I.B.8) | Defendant | *Quade* preclusion forecloses for F1.7/B1.2 (binding hail causation finding); for unevaluated windows, the post-2021 gutter replacement evidence and the family's photographic record show post-2020 storm damage; the 2020 Xactimate scope was exactly two casement windows, foreclosing "already paid" arguments for any other window. |
| Marring (§ I.A.8) | Defendant | § I.A.8(b) restores coverage for marring caused by hail; *Atwater* / *Carlson* and *contra proferentem* resolve any ambiguity for the family. |
| Corrosion / Deterioration / Decay / Rust (§ I.A.9 / I.B.9) | Defendant | Same — *Quade* causation finding forecloses; observed damage is denting, not corrosion. |
| Inherent Vice / Latent Defect (§ I.A.4 / I.B.6) | Defendant | No record support for inherent defect; Defendant's expert (Prieve) does not allege one. |
| Neglect / Lack of Maintenance (§ I.B.4) | Defendant | "New Roof" discount on the policy demonstrates prior-repair compliance; the family acted promptly when contractor identified damage. |
| Faulty / Defective Construction (§ I.B.5) | Defendant | No record support; Prieve does not allege construction defect. |

**Strategy:** Force Farmers, in answers to interrogatories and at deposition of Defendant's Rule 30(b)(6) representative, to articulate the specific factual basis for each exclusion as applied to *each* contested window. The Prieve report does not carry that load — it offers generalized causation theories, not item-by-item exclusion analysis.

### 3.2 The "fairly debatable" defense to B1 — Defendant's burden

Once B1 is added, Defendant will assert that it had a "fairly debatable" reason for denial — namely, the Prieve report. *Sela* and the line of cases applying § 604.18 treat "fairly debatable" as the dominant Prong 1 defense.

**How Plaintiffs neutralize:**

- **Prieve's foundational factual premise was objectively false.** "No hail in Eden Prairie after 2020" — disproven by the NWS report, the City permit data on the same street, the neighbor canvass, and Farmers' own subsequently-procured appraisal panel.
- **The 9/3/2024 denial pre-dated Prieve by two months.** The first denial was issued without engineering analysis. "Fairly debatable" cannot be retroactively imported to a denial that had no expert basis at issuance.
- **Farmers commissioned Prieve only after the family submitted rebuttal photos.** This sequence supports an inference that Prieve was retained to find a defense, not to develop a neutral factual record.
- **The Prieve report's narrative catalogs damage at 19+ window locations (plus a sliding door).** A report that affirmatively documents widespread observed damage and then denies hail causation invites scrutiny on the methodology gap between observation and conclusion.
- **The "engineer's report only" rule is institutional, not honest disagreement.** A supervisor-level evidentiary gate that screens out the family's NWS, permit, and canvass evidence is not a "fairly debatable" investigation — it is a structural refusal to weigh the record.

### 3.3 The Subd. 4(b) appraisal-bar defense to B1 — Defendant's burden

Defendant will argue the 9/12/2025 appraisal "resolved or confirmed" the claim and bars the § 604.18 remedy.

**How Plaintiffs neutralize (the scope reading):**

- The award lists only "F1.7" and "B1.2." It did not evaluate every window.
- Farmers' own admission of inability to consistently describe the awarded scope (9/18, 9/19, 10/14/2025) is direct evidence the award is limited.
- The umpire produced no report or estimate (Farmers' 9/24/2025 admission) — there is no record of what the panel resolved.
- Plaintiffs' photographic evidence shows damage across most of the home's windows; the panel addressed two.
- Subd. 4(b) presupposes an appraisal conducted in substantial compliance with § 65A.01 — the award's failure to "state separately actual value and loss to each item" is a non-compliance argument the family preserves.

### 3.4 Any limitations defense — Defendant's burden

If Farmers asserts the policy's 2-year suit limitation or the § 65A.26 1-year hail-policy limit, Defendant bears the burden.

**How Plaintiffs neutralize:**

- Pre-empted in Count II ¶ 48(g): the 2-year contractual limit controls; § 65A.26's older language is superseded.
- Filing within the 2-year window (before 7/13/2026) makes the timeliness question academic for the breach claim.
- Brau's 7/11/2025 boilerplate response acknowledged in writing that the suit-limitation period "may have been extended by statute or case law" — Farmers' own writing acknowledging ambiguity supports *contra proferentem* construction in the family's favor.

### 3.5 The J6944 matching defense — Defendant's burden

J6944 is an exclusion-style endorsement; Defendant bears the burden of proving its application.

**How Plaintiffs neutralize:**

- J6944(1) reaches only "property which is not damaged by the covered peril." Damaged windows are outside the gate (see E2 § 1.1 and the textual analysis).
- J6944(2) bars only "actual or perceived loss in value" — diminution in value, not coverage for replacement of damaged property.
- *Cedar Bluff Townhome Condo. Ass'n v. American Family Mut. Ins. Co.*, 857 N.W.2d 290 (Minn. 2014), supports the matching obligation under "comparable material and quality."
- Where the original-equipment manufacturer (Semco) is no longer in operation, J6944(1)(iii) "obsolescence or discontinuation" — Plaintiffs' position is that this provision applies only to undamaged property; Farmers' contrary reading collapses the gate. Ambiguity resolves under *contra proferentem*.

### 3.6 The "binding appraisal award" defense to A1 — Defendant's burden

Defendant has positioned the case as fully resolved by the appraisal. Farmers must defend the proposition that the award addressed every window the family claims was damaged.

**How Plaintiffs neutralize:**

- The award is facially limited to "F1.7" and "B1.2."
- Farmers itself cannot describe the award's scope consistently.
- The umpire produced no methodology document.
- *Quade* is a sword for Plaintiffs (binding hail causation) but cannot be transformed into a shield for Defendant covering windows the panel never examined.
- Counts I (Track 1) and II ((c) declaration) directly attack this defense.

## 4. The Concrete Offensive Moves — Phase by Phase

### 4.1 Pre-filing (now through July 2026)

1. **Retain Minnesota-licensed first-party-bad-faith counsel.** The June 11, 2025 MN Lawyer Referral Service refund is a warning that this is harder than it looks; renew the search through trial-lawyer associations and through firms with prior Minnesota first-party hail experience. *(`bowl.md` § 0.)*

2. **Resolve the open factual predicates.** Marital/domestic-partner status; household residence on 7/13/2024; Senco vs. Semco identification; MN080 endorsement text; Foster's professional history. *(`bowl.md` §§ 2–3.)*

3. **Develop the independent expert record before filing.**
   - Hail-damage forensic engineer applying the HailShapes scientific framework window-by-window.
   - Window-matching expert on the *Cedar Bluff* / J6944 question.
   - Replacement-cost / valuation expert on the Pella Reserve Traditional or Marvin Ultimate equivalents.
   - Sierra Pacific Windows availability check on the discontinued-manufacturer question.

4. **Issue a § 549.09 Subd. 1(b) offer of settlement** contemporaneous with filing. Anchor the offer at a defensible figure that captures the matching/scope-expansion theory. This locks the family's prejudgment-interest accrual and forces Farmers to respond. *(`D2.md` § 6 Item 5.)*

5. **Draft the litigation-hold letter and initial discovery package** in parallel with the complaint. Each item in `D3.md` § 3.11 and `D4.md` § 3 should appear in the first set of Rule 34 requests, ready to serve immediately after the answer.

6. **Subpoena the MN Department of Commerce file on Complaint ID 93547** under the Government Data Practices Act before filing if possible; Commerce's internal record is a free pre-litigation gift.

### 4.2 Pleading phase (filing through Rule 12)

7. **File v2 (Balanced) of the complaint by July 13, 2026.** A1 + A2 + A3 + B2 + C3, with the prayer for relief preserving § 604.18 and § 549.20 remedies via post-pleading motion. *(`00_Overview_Complaint_Drafts.md` § 4.)*

8. **Plead each candidate misrepresentation in B2 with Rule 9.02 particularity.** Date, speaker, medium, content. The Prieve "no hail in Eden Prairie after 2020" representation is the cleanest candidate.

10. **Move quickly on any Defendant Rule 12 motion.** Brief the *Force v. ITT Hartford* continuing-relationship reading to preserve C3. Brief the Rule 9.02 particularity on B2 to preserve the fraud count and the path to D1 punitive damages.

### 4.4 Motion practice (mid-case through summary judgment)

19. **File the § 604.18 Subd. 4(a) motion to amend** when discovery has produced internal Farmers documentation supporting prima facie deliberate disregard. The motion's affidavits should walk through each of the six § 72A.201 violations identified in `B1.md` § 3.1, anchored in primary-record citations. *(`bowl.md` § 9.)*

20. **File the § 549.191 motion to amend** to add a punitive-damages claim under § 549.20 once the discovery record supports clear and convincing evidence of deliberate disregard. 21. **Move for partial summary judgment on the *Quade* preclusion question.** The 9/12/2025 award's binding causation finding for F1.7 and B1.2 forecloses Farmers' six exclusions on those windows. A partial summary judgment ruling early in the case forces Farmers to defend on the unevaluated-windows track and the matching-obligation track — a narrower battlefield than the full exclusion stack.

22. **Move for partial summary judgment or remand under *Herll* / *Rymer*.** Force the panel (or a successor panel) to clarify scope. A successful remand expands or clarifies the *Quade* preclusion zone and may itself produce a higher dollar figure.

23. **Defend any Defendant summary judgment motion on B2 and C3 vigorously.** B2's reliance vulnerability and C3's "in connection with the sale" hurdle are the principal targets. The reliance reframe in `B2.md` § 3.4 (appraisal-channel reliance; litigation-delay reliance) and the *Force* continuing-relationship reading for C3 are the answers.

24. **Move in limine to exclude or limit Prieve's testimony.** Daubert-style attacks on (a) the "no hail in Eden Prairie after 2020" methodology; (b) the absence of quantitative impact analysis; (c) the unidirectional-hail generalization on the east-side siding penetration; (d) the failure to engage with NWS data available before the report was finalized.

### 4.5 Trial (if the case reaches trial)

25. **Lead with the appraisal award.** The first witness or first exhibit should be the 9/12/2025 award — Farmers' own panel — confirming hail caused damage to the windows. The narrative starts with Farmers' own factfinder agreeing with the family.

26. **Walk the jury through the 11/4/2024 denial's six exclusions.** For each, show that *Quade* forecloses application to the awarded windows, and that the underlying factual premise (Prieve's "no hail after 2020") is independently disproven.

27. **Show the supervisor-level "engineer's report only" rule.** Play the Brau February 10, 2025 voicemail. Confront Monson at trial with the policy text. The rule is the centerpiece of the bad-faith narrative.

28. **Demonstrate the missing Brau notes.** Brau personally observed dents on 9/6/2024. Farmers cannot produce his record. The family did not destroy it; Farmers did or never created it. Request the adverse-inference instruction.

29. **Show the post-award scope confusion.** Three different Farmers descriptions of the same award over four weeks. An insurer that cannot describe its own binding award has no credible defense to scope.

30. **Use the family's witnesses as the moral weight of the case.** Yibiao Lu and Jie Hu testifying about the ten-year policy relationship, the contemporaneous documentation efforts, the cold-weather neighbor canvass, the futile administrative complaints. Juries respond to insureds who did everything right.

31. **Use Farmers' own personnel against itself.** Brau on the 9/6/2024 observations; Monson on the rule; Tomes on the non-signature; Pietila on the 5/28/2025 upload that Farmers ignored.

### 4.6 Settlement strategy (running throughout)

32. **Maintain a § 549.09 offer-of-settlement posture from filing forward.** Each Defendant offer must be evaluated immediately. Each Plaintiff counter-offer must be defensible against the eventual judgment. The offer-of-settlement battlefield runs the entire case.

33. **Monitor Farmers' Rule 68 offer carefully.** A Defendant Rule 68 offer pegged near the appraisal-award figure is the principal Defendant tactic to cap Plaintiffs' post-offer cost recovery. The family must respond promptly with a defensible counter-offer that captures the matching theory.

34. **Use the post-pleading motions as settlement leverage.** A granted § 604.18 Subd. 4(a) motion materially changes the settlement calculus. A granted § 549.191 motion to add punitives raises Defendant's exposure on uninsurable damages. Each is a legitimate settlement-leverage point.

35. **Time settlement discussions to coincide with discovery wins.** A successful Rule 37 motion that produces internal Farmers documents; a Brau deposition that confirms the missing notes; a Foster deposition that admits the panel did not evaluate scope-relevant windows — each is a moment when settlement value increases.

## 5. Critical Leverage Points the Family Already Has

Five facts on the existing record carry disproportionate offensive weight. Counsel should organize the narrative around them.

### 5.1 The 9/12/2025 appraisal award is Farmers' self-disproving rebuttal of the denials

Farmers commissioned the appraisal. Farmers' appraiser was Greg Tomes. The umpire was selected through the standard § 65A.01 process. The panel — including the umpire — found hail caused damage to the awarded windows. That finding is binding on Farmers under *Quade*. It defeats the foundational premise of every causation-style exclusion in the 11/4/2024 denial. **Farmers' own subsequently-procured neutral factfinder agrees with the family on causation.** That is the strongest single offensive fact in the case.

### 5.2 The supervisor-level "engineer's report only" rule is institutional bad faith on the record

Articulated by Bradlee Monson (field claim supervisor) on 2/3/2025; confirmed in writing by Nicholas Brau's 2/10/2025 voicemail. The rule is found nowhere in the policy. It was used to refuse review of Plaintiffs' NWS data, City permits, neighbor canvass, and stormersite weather records. **A supervisor-level evidentiary rule that screens out materially relevant non-engineer evidence is not a "fairly debatable" investigation — it is a structural refusal to weigh the record under § 72A.201 and the strongest single Prong 2 fact for B1.**

### 5.3 The missing Brau 9/6/2024 inspection record

Farmers' own adjuster personally observed dents on multiple windows and measured window dimensions on 9/6/2024 — three days after the 9/3/2024 denial said "no other damages observed." Farmers' 10/10/2025 statement that "the file does not contain a report on the requested date" and Brau's 10/29/2025 statement that "all the information we can release has already been sent to you" are mutually inconsistent. Either reading hurts Farmers. **The combination supports a Rule 37 sanction, an adverse-inference instruction, and the bad-faith concealment factor under § 549.20 Subd. 3.**

## 6. Daubert and Evidentiary Attacks on Defendant's Case

### 6.1 Attack the Prieve report's foundation

- **Methodology:** No quantitative impact-force analysis; no terminal-velocity calculations; no material-specific dent-depth modeling; no peer-reviewed citations. Compare to the HailShapes scientific framework Plaintiffs will introduce.
- **Weather premise:** "Most recent hail in Eden Prairie reported in 2020" is objectively wrong on public-record data the engineer was expected to consult.
- **Directional generalization:** "Hail originates from a westerly direction" is a generalization, not an analysis of the actual 7/13–14/2024 event the NWS documents as multi-round and multi-directional.
- **Confirmation bias:** Prieve was retained after the 9/3/2024 denial and after the family submitted rebuttal photos — sequence consistent with retention for a defensive purpose, not neutral investigation.

### 6.2 Attack the Hancock photo set's evidentiary weight

The Hancock photos systematically tag every window wrap "Damaged – No." The photos themselves do not show every angle, do not capture variable lighting that reveals shallow dents, and do not include the close-up detail that subsequent inspections (Brau on 9/6/2024; Prieve on 10/9/2024) used to identify dents. Hancock's blanket "Damaged – No" conclusion is contradicted by Farmers' own subsequent inspections — making the photo set an artifact of the inspection methodology rather than a reliable factual record.

### 6.3 Attack the appraisal award's evidentiary weight as a *Defendant-side* shield

The award binds Farmers on causation under *Quade* (helps Plaintiffs) but cannot shield Farmers on scope (Plaintiffs' position). Farmers' own admission that the umpire produced no report; the inconsistent post-award descriptions; and the facial non-itemization all undercut any Defendant attempt to argue the award resolved the entire claim.

### 6.4 Attack any Defendant expert's Daubert qualifications

- Document each Defendant expert's prior insurance-industry employment record. *Carlson, et al.*-style impeachment.
- Compare the expert's methodology to the HailShapes peer-reviewed sources Plaintiffs introduce.
- Confront the expert with Farmers' own appraisal panel's contrary causation finding.

## 8. The Three Things That Win Cases Like This One

After all the procedural mechanics, the offensive strategy reduces to three propositions Plaintiffs must drive home to the court and (if reached) the jury.

### 8.1 Farmers' own appraisal panel agrees with the family

This is the cleanest fact. Farmers commissioned the panel; Farmers' appraiser (Tomes) was on it; the panel — including the neutral umpire — found hail caused damage. *Quade v. Secura Insurance*, 814 N.W.2d 703 (Minn. 2012), makes that finding binding. Every Defendant defense that depends on a contrary causation theory collapses for the awarded windows. **Lead with this.**

### 8.2 Farmers used a rule that does not exist in the policy to defeat the claim

The supervisor-level "engineer's report only" gatekeeping rule was articulated by Bradlee Monson on 2/3/2025 and confirmed in writing by Nicholas Brau on 2/10/2025. The policy contains no such requirement. The rule was used to reject the family's NWS data, City permits, neighbor canvass, and stormersite weather records — all of which independently disprove the foundational factual premise of the 11/4/2024 denial. **An insurer that imposes off-policy evidentiary gates and uses them to refuse review of disproving evidence is not handling a claim in good faith. That is the centerpiece of the bad-faith narrative under § 604.18 and § 549.20.**

### 8.3 Farmers cannot produce its own investigation record

Farmers' own adjuster personally observed dents and measured windows on 9/6/2024. Farmers stated on 10/10/2025 that no report exists; Brau personally refused to release notes on 10/29/2025. The two statements are mutually inconsistent. The umpire produced no report. The Hancock workproduct beyond the photo set has not been produced. Farmers cannot describe the awarded scope consistently. **An insurer that loses or never creates the evidentiary record of its own investigation is not entitled to the benefit of the doubt on what that record would have shown. That is the doctrinal basis for adverse-inference instructions, Rule 37 sanctions, and the spoliation-adjacent factor under § 549.20 Subd. 3.**

These three propositions, anchored in primary-record citations, are the offensive playbook in compressed form. Every motion, deposition, written submission, and trial argument should serve at least one of them.
