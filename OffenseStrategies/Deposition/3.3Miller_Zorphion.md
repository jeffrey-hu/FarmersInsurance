## 1. Strategy — Why This Deposition

Zorphion Miller is the **lead Farmers correspondent on the post-appraisal email chain**. The 9/18/2025, 9/19/2025, and 10/14/2025 emails describing the awarded windows were sent from her. Specifically: on 9/18 she described the awarded windows as "front right, and the rear elevation in the dining room"; on 9/19 she acknowledged the "dining room" reference was a mistake; on 10/14 she repeated the original "dining room" description verbatim.

Three different descriptions of the same award over four weeks. Zorphion Miller is the institutional voice of Farmers' inability to describe its own binding award.

The deposition serves to:

1. **Lock in the three inconsistent descriptions on the record.** Force Miller to confirm she sent each email; force her to confirm each description's substance; force her to confirm the descriptions differ.

2. **Identify the source of each description.** Whom did she rely on — Brau? Tomes? The appraisal file? The award form? Each source has its own implications. If she relied on the award form alone, that confirms the codes are facially uninterpretable. If she relied on Brau or Tomes, that supports a follow-up examination.

3. **Develop the Subd. 4(b) appraisal-bar defense record.** If Farmers cannot describe the awarded scope consistently, Farmers cannot establish that the appraisal "resolved" the claim.

4. **Develop the *Herll*/*Rymer* remand record.** The inconsistency is the family's strongest single piece of remand-supporting evidence.

5. **Document the 10/21/2025 family-asks-about-scope-gap response.** Miller (or another correspondent) replied: "The claim was settled per the binding appraisal award received. The loss is under your deductible. If you need information as to why additional windows were not included in the award then you will need to contact your appraiser to obtain that information." That is § 72A.201 Subd. 6(d) violation evidence — refusing to engage on the merits.

This is a short, focused deposition. Scope is narrow.

## 7. Prep Memo (Final Synthesis)

### 7.1 The One Sentence

Get Miller to confirm under oath that she sent the 9/18, 9/19, and 10/14/2025 emails; that each description is inconsistent with the others; and that she cannot identify a single accurate description of the awarded scope.

### 7.2 The Two Critical Locks

1. **The three-inconsistent-descriptions lock.** Sworn confirmation by the author. This is *Herll*/*Rymer* remand evidence on the cleanest possible footing — Farmers' own correspondent under oath.
