## 1. Strategy — Why This Deposition

Twonda Miller appears in the record as a Farmers correspondent on the **10/28/2025 family email reporting active window leaks on a rainy day**. She was copied (along with Zorphion Miller and Jed Jones) when the family wrote to report ongoing water intrusion and to ask again why most damaged windows were not covered.

The deposition is short and supporting. Its purposes:

1. **Document Farmers' awareness of ongoing water-intrusion damage as of 10/28/2025** — relevant to A1 consequential damages and to the continuing-breach narrative.

2. **Document Farmers' institutional response (or non-response) to the 10/28/2025 leak report** — § 72A.201 Subd. 6(d) violation if no merits engagement.

3. **Identify the chain of internal communications about the 10/28/2025 leak report.**

4. **Develop the institutional record on post-appraisal correspondence**.

This deposition is shortest of the post-appraisal correspondents. Twonda Miller may have a narrower role than Zorphion Miller. The deposition is primarily to lock in facts and to make the institutional record complete; it is not a primary battlefield.
