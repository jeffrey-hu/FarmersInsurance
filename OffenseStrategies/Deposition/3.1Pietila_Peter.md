## 1. Strategy — Why This Deposition

Pietila is the family's local Farmers agent. He sold them the policy; he is their point of contact for non-claim-handling questions; he has been **the most cooperative Farmers-affiliated person on the record**. He met with the family in September 2024 to advise on documenting damage. He met with them on 1/31/2025 and provided Bradlee Monson's contact information. He met with them on 5/28/2025 and **uploaded the NWS storm article and Interactive Hail Maps data to the claim portal himself**. On 7/11/2025 he confirmed in writing: "We attached it to the claim, but I haven't got any update. Nic would be the best contact."

Pietila is a **cooperative witness whose testimony helps the family**. The deposition's purpose is to:

1. **Lock in the 5/28/2025 upload as documented**. Confirm under oath that Pietila personally uploaded the NWS and Interactive Hail Maps evidence to the claim portal and asked the claim team to review.

2. **Document Farmers' non-response to the upload**. Pietila's 7/11/2025 "haven't got any update" is direct, in-writing evidence that Farmers ignored evidence submitted through its own channels — § 72A.201 Subd. 5 and Subd. 6(d) violation.

3. **Establish the agency relationship and what Pietila told the family**. Pietila advised the family on 9/11/2024 to document damage; that advice is part of the contractual-performance record under A1.

4. **Develop the circular-refusal narrative**. Pietila advised the family to contact Prieve directly; Prieve refused; Brau refused. That circular pattern is § 72A.201 evidence.

5. **Develop institutional knowledge of the engineer-only rule**. Did Pietila ever hear Monson or anyone else articulate the rule? Was Pietila told to follow the rule when communicating with the family?

The strategic posture: **friendly examination, focused on locking in helpful facts**. This is not a confrontational deposition. The risk is that Pietila will be deposed by Farmers' counsel and asked questions that minimize his role; the family's deposition should establish the helpful record first.

## 7. Prep Memo (Final Synthesis)

### 7.1 The One Sentence

Get Pietila to confirm under oath that on 5/28/2025 he personally uploaded the NWS article and Interactive Hail Maps data to the Farmers claim portal and asked the claim team to review, and that he received no substantive response from Farmers despite his own 7/11/2025 in-writing check on the family's behalf.

### 7.2 The Three Critical Locks

1. **The 5/28/2025 upload lock.** Documents that the family submitted evidence through Farmers' own channels — the claim portal — and via Farmers' own agent. Farmers cannot claim it never received the evidence.

2. **The non-response lock.** Pietila's 7/11/2025 "haven't got any update" is in writing. Even with Farmers' own agent acting as the delivery channel, the claim team did not engage. § 72A.201 Subd. 5 and Subd. 6(d) violation.

3. **The institutional knowledge lock.** Pietila either heard the engineer-only rule articulated (institutional knowledge that helps the family) or did not (which raises the question of why an agent was not informed of the substantive rule that controlled the claim).

### 7.3 Do Not Open These Doors

- Do not let Pietila distance himself from his own 7/11/2025 statement.
- Do not invite Pietila to opine on bad faith.
- Do not push him on litigation strategy.

### 7.4 Coordination With Other Depositions

- Schedule after Brau and Monson (so Pietila's testimony fills institutional gaps the Farmers personnel testified to).
- Schedule before Rule 30(b)(6) so institutional questions can incorporate Pietila's testimony.
