## 1. Strategy — Why This Deposition

Dudley signed the **9/3/2024 first denial letter** — the document that locked Farmers into a baseline position the rest of the case has been walking away from. The 9/3 letter said "interior water damage around your windows was observed. There were no other damages observed. There was no opening created by the direct force of wind or hail that could have occurred and allowed water to enter and cause immediate damage." That is a categorical "no exterior damage exists anywhere on the home" statement. It was issued **before any forensic engineer was engaged** — Prieve wasn't retained until weeks later.

Dudley's deposition serves to:

1. **Establish that the 9/3/2024 denial was issued without engineering analysis** — defeating the later "fairly debatable" defense to bad faith. *Sela* and the "fairly debatable" line of cases require the insurer to identify a reasonable basis at the time of denial; the 9/3 denial cannot be retroactively bootstrapped to Prieve.

2. **Lock in the categorical "no other damages observed" finding.** This is the baseline that everything else (Brau's 9/6 dent observations; the 9/12/2025 appraisal award; Prieve's own catalog of damage at 12+ window locations) contradicts. Dudley's deposition makes clear that Farmers, through its catastrophe claims rep with signing authority, said the home had no damage.

3. **Document the shifting-rationale picture.** Dudley's "no opening / interior water damage exclusion" theory of 9/3/2024 is incompatible with Prieve's "wear/tear / 2020-already-paid" theory of 11/4/2024. Forced articulation of why the theories shifted is § 72A.201 Subd. 5 (failure to maintain consistent claim-handling practices) evidence.

4. **Identify the actual basis of the 9/3 denial.** Was it Hancock's photo set alone? Dudley's personal review? Supervisor instruction? An automatic-denial protocol for hail claims? Each answer feeds the B1 record.

5. **Develop the institutional record on catastrophe-team denial practices.** Dudley is a catastrophe claims rep — the people Farmers deploys after large storm events. His testimony on the catastrophe-team's standard operating procedures supports C3 pattern-of-practice.

Dudley is a lower-priority deposition than Brau or Monson — but he is the foundation witness on the first denial, and a clean foundation here makes everything downstream easier.

## 6. Anticipated Objections

Standard objection responses (see Brau memo § 6). Dudley-specific:

- **"Calls for legal conclusion" on whether the 9/3 denial was "premature."** Reframe: ask about the timing — "On 9/3/2024, when you signed the denial, had any forensic engineer been engaged on this claim?" That is a factual question, not a legal conclusion.
- **"Privilege" on internal review communications.** If post-litigation-foreseeability, accept; if pre-litigation business communications, pursue.
- **"Cumulative" if Dudley's testimony seems to overlap with Hancock's Grimm.** Counter: Dudley signed the letter, Grimm did not; the question is what Dudley knew and what Dudley decided.
- **Speaking objections.** Object on the record per Rule 30.04(c).

## 7. Prep Memo (Final Synthesis)

### 7.1 The One Sentence

Get Dudley to admit, on the record, that he signed the 9/3/2024 denial declaring "no other damages observed" before any forensic engineer had been engaged on the claim.

### 7.2 The Three Critical Locks

1. **The pre-engineer denial lock.** No forensic engineer was engaged on 9/3/2024. The denial rests entirely on Hancock's photo set and Dudley's review. This forecloses retroactively bootstrapping the denial to Prieve.

2. **The categorical "no damage" lock.** "There were no other damages observed" is unambiguous. Brau's 9/6 observations contradict it. The 9/12/2025 appraisal contradicts it. Prieve's own catalog of dents at 12+ locations contradicts it. Dudley's denial is the baseline that every subsequent Farmers position walks away from.

3. **The shifting-rationale lock.** 9/3/2024: "no other damages." 11/4/2024: "damage exists but is wear/tear / already-paid in 2020." These are factually incompatible. Forced acknowledgment by Dudley that the rationales shifted is § 72A.201 evidence and B1 Prong 2 evidence.

### 7.3 Do Not Open These Doors

- Do not argue with Dudley about whether Hancock's photos were thorough — let Hancock's Grimm defend Hancock's work.
- Do not let Dudley opine on the appraisal — he was off the file by then.
- Do not invite Dudley to testify to engineering matters; he is not an engineer.

### 7.4 Three Things Dudley Will Try to Do

1. **Claim the 9/3 denial was procedural, not substantive.** "It was a standard catastrophe-team initial denial; we reopen all the time." Response: the letter is on Farmers letterhead, signed by Dudley, with substantive findings ("no other damages observed") and 9 cited exclusions. It is a denial, not a placeholder.

2. **Hide behind Hancock.** "I relied on Hancock; they're the field inspectors." Response: Dudley signed the denial; that is the decision. Hancock's failure to find damage (which Brau and Prieve both later observed) does not absolve Dudley.

3. **Claim institutional knowledge of post-9/3 events.** "After Brau inspected, we issued a more accurate finding in November." Response: the 9/3 denial was never withdrawn; it stayed on the file; the 11/4 denial rests on incompatible factual premises.

### 7.5 Coordination With Other Depositions

- Schedule after Brau and Monson; before Grimm (so Grimm can be examined on the basis Hancock supplied to Dudley).
- Coordinate with Rule 30(b)(6) testimony on catastrophe-team policies.
- The deposition feeds B4 (estoppel/waiver) on the shifting rationales and B1 Prong 1 (no reasonable basis at time of denial).

### 7.6 Post-Deposition Action Items

- Cross-reference Dudley's testimony with the produced drafts of the 9/3 denial (Exhibit 4) for any inconsistency that supports a follow-up Rule 37 motion.
