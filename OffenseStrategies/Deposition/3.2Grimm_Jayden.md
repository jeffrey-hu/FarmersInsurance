## 1. Strategy — Why This Deposition

Grimm was the first inspector — Hancock Claims Consultants on 8/29/2024. His photo set (`EventDocuments/FarmersDenyLetter_09_03_2024.pdf` pp. 5–74) became the entire evidentiary basis for Christian Dudley's 9/3/2024 denial. Every window wrap is tagged "Damaged – No." Grimm did not write a narrative report (as far as has been produced); the photo set alone is what Hancock delivered.

The Grimm deposition serves to:

1. **Test the methodology of the blanket "Damaged – No" conclusion**. How did Grimm reach that conclusion? What did he look at? What did he miss?

2. **Document the contradiction with Brau's 9/6/2024 observations and Prieve's 10/9/2024 catalog of dents**. The same property; the same windows; three inspections; three different conclusions about what is on the home. Grimm's deposition establishes that Hancock's photo set is an artifact of the inspection methodology, not a reliable factual record.

3. **Support the C3 pattern-of-practice case**. How often does Hancock find "no damage" in Minnesota hail-claim inspections? What is Hancock's standard methodology? Pattern evidence on Hancock supports the institutional-Farmers picture.

4. **Build the Daubert foundation on Hancock's evidentiary weight at trial**. If Grimm has no engineering credentials, no quantitative methodology, and his "Damaged – No" tags were applied without close-up examination, his photos lose evidentiary force.

5. **Develop the Hancock retention pattern with Farmers**. How many Minnesota engagements has Hancock had with Farmers since 2020? What is the standard process? What outcomes?

Grimm is a routine third-party witness. The deposition is focused but should not be exhaustive — Grimm's photo set speaks for itself, and the family's case is stronger from Brau, Monson, Prieve, and the appraisal record. This deposition is supporting, not primary.

## 7. Prep Memo (Final Synthesis)

### 7.1 The One Sentence

Get Grimm to admit, on the record, that he tagged each window wrap "Damaged – No" without close-up examination, without measurement, without any methodology he can defend on cross-examination — and that Farmers' own subsequent inspections (Brau on 9/6; Prieve on 10/9) and the 9/12/2025 appraisal panel reached different conclusions on the same property.

### 7.2 The Three Critical Locks

1. **The methodology gap lock.** Grimm's inspection was photographic, not engineering. The blanket "Damaged – No" tags rest on visual observation without close-up examination or measurement. This is a Daubert-style attack on Hancock's evidentiary weight.

2. **The cross-inspection inconsistency lock.** The same windows photographed by Grimm on 8/29 were inspected by Brau on 9/6 (dents observed), Prieve on 10/9 (dents catalogued at 19+ locations), and the appraisal panel on 9/12/2025 (hail causation found for F1.7 and B1.2). Grimm's photos cannot be reconciled with three subsequent inspections.

3. **The pattern-of-practice lock.** Hancock's frequency of "no damage" findings on Minnesota Farmers hail inspections supports the C3 institutional-pattern record.
