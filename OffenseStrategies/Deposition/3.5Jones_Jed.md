## 1. Strategy — Why This Deposition

Jed Jones appears on the post-appraisal correspondence chain — copied along with Zorphion Miller and Twonda Miller on the **10/28/2025 family email reporting active window leaks** and asking why most damaged windows were not covered. Jones's specific role within Farmers' post-appraisal correspondence handling is to be determined through document discovery and the deposition itself.

The deposition serves to:

1. **Complete the institutional record on the post-appraisal correspondence.** Z. Miller anchors the inconsistent scope descriptions; T. Miller documents the 10/28 leak report awareness; Jones rounds out the institutional chain.

2. **Identify Jones's specific role.** Each Farmers correspondent had some role; some are signatories, some are reviewers, some are routers. Jones's role bears on what he knew and when.

3. **Develop any unique facts Jones has** that Z. Miller and T. Miller do not — internal communications, supervisor review, file-handling protocols.

4. **Lock in Jones's awareness of the awarded-scope inconsistencies.** Even if Jones did not author the 9/18, 9/19, 10/14/2025 emails, he was part of the institutional correspondence cluster and likely reviewed or was copied.

This is the shortest of the three post-appraisal correspondent depositions. Scope is focused.

## 7. Prep Memo (Final Synthesis)

### 7.1 The One Sentence

Get Jones to confirm under oath his role on the post-appraisal correspondence cluster, his awareness of the inconsistent awarded-scope descriptions, and the institutional process (or absence of process) for engaging the family on the merits after the 9/12/2025 award.

### 7.2 The Two Critical Locks

1. **The institutional-cluster awareness lock.** Jones knew of the inconsistent descriptions and the refusal-to-engage on scope. § 72A.201 Subd. 6(d) violation evidence.

2. **The 10/21 reply attribution lock.** If Jones drafted, reviewed, or approved the 10/21 reply directing the family to their own appraiser for the scope explanation, that is direct attribution of the institutional refusal-to-engage.

### 7.3 Coordination With Other Depositions

- Schedule jointly with or shortly after Z. Miller and T. Miller.
- The combined testimony of Z. Miller, T. Miller, and Jones anchors the post-appraisal § 72A.201 violation cluster in the B1 affidavit.

### 7.4 Post-Deposition Action Items

- Use Jones's testimony in the B1 Subd. 4(a) affidavit and in the *Herll*/*Rymer* remand briefing.
- Cross-reference Jones's testimony with Z. Miller and T. Miller for the complete institutional chain.

### 7.5 Strategic Note

Jones is the most peripheral of the three post-appraisal correspondents identified in the case record. The deposition may discover that his role was minimal — a CC recipient, not a substantive participant. If so, the deposition is short and the family can move on. If Jones turns out to have a more substantive role (e.g., supervisor or approver), the scope expands. Adjust on the fly based on initial answers to the role-and-responsibilities questions in Block 1.
