## 1. Strategy — Why This Deposition

Prieve is the **engineer Farmers commissioned to provide the technical basis for the 11/4/2024 denial**. His report (File #241008) is the principal substantive justification for Farmers' second denial. The report contains a foundational factual error — "most recent hail in Eden Prairie reported in 2020" — that is directly disproven by the NWS Twin Cities/Chanhassen storm report, City of Eden Prairie permits EP187829 and EP190835, the 14-of-19 neighbor canvass, and Farmers' own subsequent 9/12/2025 appraisal award. The report's methodology has no quantitative impact-force analysis, no terminal-velocity calculations, no material-specific dent-depth modeling, no peer-reviewed citations — none of the rigor the HailShapes scientific framework treats as standard.

This deposition serves to:

1. **Destroy the "fairly debatable" defense to bad faith.** Prieve's report is Farmers' principal Prong 1 defense. A deposition that demonstrates the report's methodological deficiencies and factual errors deprives Farmers of a "reasonable basis" for denial.

2. **Set up the Daubert motion to exclude or limit Prieve's testimony at trial.** Each methodological gap exposed in the deposition is a Daubert ground.

3. **Establish the "no hail after 2020" assertion as a false statement of fact.** This is the cleanest of the three candidate B2 misrepresentations. Forced articulation of the data sources Prieve used (and the data sources he should have but did not consult) supports the falsity element.

4. **Document Prieve's retention and management by Farmers.** The fact-finding on retention timing (after the family submitted rebuttal photos), payment, communications with Farmers, and the draft-review process supports the inference that Prieve was retained for a defensive purpose.

5. **Develop the C3 pattern-of-practice record.** Prieve's other Minnesota engagements with Farmers since 2020 — across how many claims, with what outcomes — anchor the institutional pattern.

6. **Lock in the factual content of the Prieve report.** Prieve's own narrative catalogs dents at 12+ window locations. Force Prieve to confirm those observations on the record. That confirmation is contradictory evidence Farmers cannot escape — the engineer Farmers paid documented damage on most of the home's windows.

Prieve will resist on multiple fronts: expert-witness protection, work-product, the "this is my professional opinion" deflection, and the "I wasn't asked to evaluate that" punt. The deposition must penetrate each.

## 6. Anticipated Objections

### 6.1 Privilege and Work-Product

Prieve was retained by Farmers; Farmers may assert work-product over draft reports, internal communications, and certain analyses. Counter:

- **Final report is not protected.** Already produced.
- **Underlying facts are not protected.** Field observations, measurements, photos, data-source consultations are facts.
- **Draft reports created for an insurance-claim decision (not in anticipation of litigation) are not protected.** The denial was a business decision, not a litigation-driven evaluation. Push hard on this.
- **Once a witness is disclosed as a testifying expert, the work-product protection narrows.** If Farmers designates Prieve as a trial expert, his entire file becomes discoverable under Minn. R. Civ. P. 26.01(b)(2).

### 6.2 Expert-Witness Status

Two possibilities:
- **Prieve as fact witness only.** His report is a pre-litigation business document; he is a fact witness about what he observed and concluded. Most discovery is allowed.
- **Prieve as Farmers' designated trial expert.** His file is discoverable; his methodology is fully subject to Daubert.

Demand clarification at the meet-and-confer. If Farmers refuses to designate, treat as fact witness for purposes of deposition scope; preserve right to recall for full expert deposition if subsequently designated.

### 6.3 Other Anticipated Objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Calls for opinion outside scope of retention" | Counter: Prieve's report makes specific factual assertions ("no hail after 2020") — those are within his scope and admissible |
| "Hypothetical" on the multi-directional hail question | Reframe with specific NWS data |
| "Argumentative" on the 2020 reconciliation | Reframe as factual: "How many windows did Farmers' 2020 Xactimate approve?" "How many dented windows does your report document?" |
| "Compound" on data-source questions | Break into one source per question |
| Confidentiality / trade-secret on Prieve Engineering's business records | Accept protective-order designations; pursue underlying facts |

## 7. Prep Memo (Final Synthesis)

### 7.1 The One Sentence

Get Prieve to admit, on the record, that (a) his "no hail in Eden Prairie after 2020" statement is contradicted by public records he did not consult; (b) his methodology did not include quantitative impact-force analysis, terminal-velocity calculations, material-specific dent-depth modeling, or peer-reviewed citations; and (c) his own report documents dents at 12+ window locations that his "wear/tear / 2020-already-paid" conclusion cannot reconcile with Farmers' 2020 Xactimate scope.

### 7.2 The Three Critical Locks

1. **The factually wrong assertion lock.** "No hail in Eden Prairie after 2020" is disprovable. Force Prieve to either defend the assertion (against NWS, City permits, neighbor canvass, and the 9/12/2025 appraisal award) or concede that he was wrong. Either is good.

2. **The methodology lock.** No quantitative impact-force analysis, no terminal-velocity calculations, no material-specific modeling, no peer-reviewed citations. Each missing piece is a Daubert ground.

3. **The damage-existence lock.** Prieve's own report catalogs dents at 12+ locations. That is uncontested factual record. The "wear/tear / 2020" theory cannot accommodate dents at 12+ locations when the 2020 Xactimate approved 2 windows.

### 7.3 Do Not Open These Doors

- Do not let Prieve give a long lecture on hail science; he gets to give the answer to the question asked.
- Do not let Prieve characterize the family's evidence as inadequate without explaining the specific methodology that would have been adequate.
- Do not ask "why" questions until the foundation is locked in.
- Do not let Prieve volunteer "expert opinion" beyond the scope of his report.

### 7.4 Three Things Prieve Will Try to Do

1. **Defend "no hail after 2020" by qualifying.** "I meant 'no hail of consequence' or 'no reported hail in a specific database.'" Response: read his exact words back. "Most recent hail in Eden Prairie reported in 2020." That is a categorical statement. Force defense or retraction.

2. **Defend the methodology by reference to "standard industry practice."** Response: ask for the specific industry guideline or standard he relied on. Force identification or concession.

3. **Reconcile the 12+ damage observations with the 2020 attribution by speculation.** "The damage might have accumulated over multiple storms." Response: Farmers' 2020 Xactimate approved 2 windows. Show Prieve Exhibit 9. Where, then, did the dents on the other 10+ windows come from if not from a post-2020 event Prieve says didn't happen.

Even partial admissions on each of the three critical locks destroy Farmers' "fairly debatable" defense and elevate the case from contract dispute to bad-faith.
