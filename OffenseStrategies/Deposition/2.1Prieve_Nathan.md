## 1. Strategy — Why This Deposition

Prieve is the **engineer Farmers commissioned to provide the technical basis for the 11/4/2024 denial**. His report (File #241008) is the principal substantive justification for Farmers' second denial. The report contains a foundational factual error — "most recent hail in Eden Prairie reported in 2020" — that is directly disproven by the NWS Twin Cities/Chanhassen storm report, City of Eden Prairie permits EP187829 and EP190835, the 14-of-19 neighbor canvass, and Farmers' own subsequent 9/12/2025 appraisal award. The report's methodology has no quantitative impact-force analysis, no terminal-velocity calculations, no material-specific dent-depth modeling, no peer-reviewed citations — none of the rigor the HailShapes scientific framework treats as standard.

This deposition serves to:

1. **Destroy the "fairly debatable" defense to bad faith.** Prieve's report is Farmers' principal Prong 1 defense. A deposition that demonstrates the report's methodological deficiencies and factual errors deprives Farmers of a "reasonable basis" for denial.

2. **Set up the Daubert motion to exclude or limit Prieve's testimony at trial.** Each methodological gap exposed in the deposition is a Daubert ground.

3. **Establish the "no hail after 2020" assertion as a false statement of fact.** This is the cleanest of the three candidate B2 misrepresentations. Forced articulation of the data sources Prieve used (and the data sources he should have but did not consult) supports the falsity element.

4. **Document Prieve's retention and management by Farmers.** The fact-finding on retention timing (after the family submitted rebuttal photos), payment, communications with Farmers, and the draft-review process supports the inference that Prieve was retained for a defensive purpose.

5. **Develop the C3 pattern-of-practice record.** Prieve's other Minnesota engagements with Farmers since 2020 — across how many claims, with what outcomes — anchor the institutional pattern.

6. **Lock in the factual content of the Prieve report.** Prieve's own narrative catalogs dents at 25+ window locations (plus a sliding door). Force Prieve to confirm those observations on the record. That confirmation is contradictory evidence Farmers cannot escape — the engineer Farmers paid documented damage on most of the home's windows.

Prieve will resist on multiple fronts: expert-witness protection, work-product, the "this is my professional opinion" deflection, and the "I wasn't asked to evaluate that" punt. The deposition must penetrate each.

### 2.6.A The Garage-Window Sentence — Prieve's Own Diagnostic for Hail Damage (NEW — Critical Sequence)

The single most useful sentence in the Prieve report appears on page 6 (page 9 of the combined 11/4/2024 denial-letter PDF), in the discussion of the rear/east garage window:

> "A 2 unit window appears at the rear/east side of the garage adjacent to a shed. Various scrape marks appear in the frame of the window not consistent with hail. **No smooth or rounded dents were observed in the frame or sashes.**"

Prieve uses the *absence* of "smooth or rounded dents" at the garage window as the reason to rule out hail there. The mirror proposition — that the *presence* of smooth or rounded dents would indicate hail — is Prieve's implicit diagnostic. The deposition must lock that diagnostic in, then walk Prieve through the rest of his own report to convict the report's conclusion.

The 7-step cross-examination sequence is the lock:

1. **Read aloud the garage-window sentence.** Hand Prieve Exhibit 3 (the report) flagged at page 6. Read the sentence verbatim. Confirm it is his report and he wrote it.
2. **Confirm the operative diagnostic.** Ask: "At the rear/east garage window, you ruled out hail causation in part because no smooth or rounded dents were observed. Is that correct?" Then: "If smooth or rounded dents had been observed at the garage window, would that have been consistent with hail causation?"
3. **Confirm the standard is general, not local.** Ask: "Is the 'smooth or rounded' diagnostic the same standard you applied at every window you evaluated on the property?" Pin him to "yes." If "no," ask which windows used a different standard, why, and where in the report he disclosed the variation.
4. **Walk to page 4 — front/west triple window.** Hand Prieve Exhibit 3 flagged at page 4. Read aloud: "A triple window unit appears at the main level to the north of the main entry as shown in photograph A-38. **Rounded dents** were observed at sashes of this window unit." Confirm: he wrote those words; the dents were rounded; rounded dents are consistent with hail under his diagnostic.
5. **Walk to page 5 — north porch four-unit window.** Hand Prieve Exhibit 3 flagged at page 5. Read aloud: "Two dents appear at the lower left corner of the left casement **which are generally smooth**." Confirm: he wrote those words; the dents were generally smooth; generally smooth dents are consistent with hail under his diagnostic.
6. **Demand the causation.** Ask: "By the diagnostic you applied at the rear/east garage window, what caused the rounded dents at the front/west triple window and the generally smooth dents at the north porch four-unit window?" The only methodologically honest answer is "hail." If Prieve says "the 2020 storm," go to step 7.
7. **Defeat the 2020 attribution.** Ask: "How do you distinguish, in geometry or signature, a smooth or rounded dent caused by 2020 hail from a smooth or rounded dent caused by 7/13/2024 hail?" Force Prieve to identify a methodological basis. He has none. Then: "The 2020 storm produced hail of 1 to 2 inches per your Weather Records section. The 7/13–7/14/2024 storm produced hail up to 3.5 to 4 inches across east-central Minnesota per the National Weather Service. Under your diagnostic, which storm is more likely to have caused the rounded dents you observed?" There is no good answer.

After the sequence, anchor with:

- Where in the Prieve report is the "smooth or rounded" diagnostic defined or supported with peer-reviewed citation? (Answer: nowhere.)
- Where in the Prieve report is the "scrape marks inside dents → blunt object other than hail" framework defined or supported with peer-reviewed citation? (Answer: nowhere.)
- Where in the HailShapes peer-reviewed literature (Exhibit 8) is the proposition that hailstones produce only smooth or rounded dents supported? (Answer: it is not — the literature establishes the opposite.)
- Where in the HailShapes literature is the proposition that scrape marks rule out hail supported? (Answer: it is not — wind-driven oblique-angle hail impacts routinely produce scraping signatures.)

**The strategic value.** The garage-window sentence is a Daubert ground (the diagnostic exists, is operative, and has no peer-reviewed support), a damage-existence concession (Prieve's own words document hail-signature dents on at least two windows), a *Quade* alignment (the appraisal panel awarded the front/west window where Prieve documented rounded dents), and a fraud-element falsity exhibit (the diagnostic Prieve uses to deny is the diagnostic his own observations satisfy). Every later motion can cite this single sentence.

## 6. Anticipated Objections

### 6.1 Privilege and Work-Product

Prieve was retained by Farmers; Farmers may assert work-product over draft reports, internal communications, and certain analyses. Counter:

- **Final report is not protected.** Already produced.
- **Underlying facts are not protected.** Field observations, measurements, photos, data-source consultations are facts.
