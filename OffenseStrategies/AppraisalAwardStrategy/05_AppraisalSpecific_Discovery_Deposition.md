# Appraisal-Specific Discovery and Deposition

**Purpose.** Identify the discovery requests, third-party subpoenas, and deposition topics specifically scoped to Count III. These items supplement (and in some places refine) the general discovery plan in `OffenseStrategies/Discovery/DiscoveryPlan.md` and the deposition outlines in `OffenseStrategies/Deposition/2.2Foster_John.md`, `2.3Roos_Wade.md`, and `2.4Tomes_Greg.md`.

**Why a Count III-specific layer exists.** The general discovery plan targets the bad-faith and breach-of-contract case. The Count III motion (per `04_MotionTactics.md`) requires a tighter, faster pre-motion record: (a) decoding F1.7 and B1.2; (b) confirming the umpire's missing methodology; (c) documenting the inconsistent-scope descriptions; (d) developing the matching-cost methodology for the remand order. Each of those items has discovery requirements that the general plan addresses only generally. This file makes them specific.

## 2. Decoding F1.7 and B1.2

### 2.1 The information needed

Confirmed identification of each awarded window by:
- Building elevation (north, south, east, west);
- Story / floor (basement, main, upper);
- Sash count and configuration;
- Manufacturer name (Senco/Semco vs. other) and visible stamp date(s);
- Cross-reference to one or more photographs in Prieve Appendix A.

Best evidence will come from whichever party of the panel kept a working index — likely Pure Construction (which prepared the appraisal scope) or Foster (the insured's appraiser, who must have used a code system in his evaluation).

### 2.2 Rule 45 subpoena targets

**Pure Construction (Zach Sexe, Jade).** Already in the general plan at `DiscoveryPlan.md` § 6.1. Specific Count III items to add:
- The window-by-window inventory or scope of work prepared by Pure Construction's "window expert" between 5/14/2025 and 8/3/2025.
- Any code-key or designation system used to identify individual windows (the "F1" / "B1" series of codes likely originates here).
- All communications between Pure Construction and Foster regarding window identification and code assignment.
- All communications between Pure Construction and the family regarding the windows to include in the appraisal demand.

**John Foster.** Already in the general plan. Specific Count III items to add:
- Any worksheet, spreadsheet, or document Foster used during the 9/12/2025 inspection identifying individual windows by code or location.
- Foster's 9/12 field notes, including any contemporaneous identification of F1.7 and B1.2.
- Foster's working file on the post-inspection deliberations.
- The basis on which Foster signed an award lumping F1.7 and B1.2 into a single $12,800 figure.

**Wade Roos.** Already in the general plan. Specific Count III items to add:
- Any document Roos created or received that identifies F1.7 and B1.2 by location or photographic cross-reference.
- Roos's understanding, at the time of signing, of which windows the $12,800 figure covered.

**Greg Tomes.** Already in the general plan. Specific Count III items to add:
- Tomes's working file, including any document Tomes received from Pure Construction or Foster identifying F1.7 and B1.2.
- The basis for Tomes's decision not to sign the award (was disagreement about scope, valuation, both, or other?).

### 2.3 Rule 33 interrogatory to Farmers

A targeted interrogatory in addition to those already in `DiscoveryPlan.md`:

> **INT-CIII.1.** Identify by location (elevation, story, sash count, manufacturer stamp date) each window covered by the designations "F1.7" and "B1.2" in the September 12, 2025 Appraisal Award. State separately, for each such window, the Replacement Cost Value and Actual Cash Value attributed to that window in the $12,800 / $6,400 lump-sum figures.

If Farmers cannot answer, the answer itself becomes a *Herll* exhibit ("Defendant cannot describe its own binding award").

>
> **RFA-CIII.2.** The designation "B1.2" in the September 12, 2025 Appraisal Award refers to the three-sash window in the family room facing the back of the home at 17756 George Moran Dr.
>
> **RFA-CIII.3.** The $12,800 Replacement Cost Value figure in the September 12, 2025 Appraisal Award is a single lump-sum value covering the two windows identified as "F1.7" and "B1.2" collectively, not a separate per-window value.
>
> **RFA-CIII.4.** The September 12, 2025 Appraisal Award does not state separately the Replacement Cost Value attributable to the window identified as "F1.7" and the Replacement Cost Value attributable to the window identified as "B1.2."

If Farmers admits these RFAs, the non-itemization argument writes itself. If Farmers denies, the denials are impeachable on the face of the award and through the F1.7 / B1.2 decoding evidence.

## 6. Tomes's Non-Signature

### 6.1 Background

Farmers' appraiser Greg Tomes did not sign the 9/12/2025 award. Under § 65A.01 Subd. 3 the award is procedurally valid on any two of three signatures, so the non-signature did not invalidate the award. But the non-signature is evidence — it suggests Tomes disagreed substantively with the award. The disagreement could be about scope, valuation, methodology, or causation. Each possibility has different implications.

### 6.2 Rule 45 subpoena to Tomes

Already in the general plan. Specific Count III items to add:

- Tomes's working file on the appraisal: notes, photographs, measurements, sketches, methodology.
- Tomes's communications with Roos, Foster, Farmers, and Pure Construction.
- Any internal Tomes-Farmers communications about the appraisal scope, methodology, or outcome.
- The basis on which Tomes declined to sign the award.
- Tomes's prior Minnesota appraisal engagements as Farmers' party-appraiser 2020–present, with sign-vs.-no-sign tally and the reasons for any non-signatures.

### 6.3 Rule 33 interrogatory

## 10. The Two Single Most Important Discovery Outputs

If everything else is set aside, the Tier 1 motion depends on two outputs:

**Output 1 — The F1.7 / B1.2 decoding key.** Without it, the family's ambiguity argument is "the codes are undefined." With it (showing F1.7 = window X, B1.2 = window Y, and identifying 17 other hail-signature windows Prieve documented that the panel did not evaluate), the family's ambiguity argument becomes "Farmers' own appraiser/contractor identified 19 windows, the panel awarded 2 of them, and the panel never explained what happened to the rest."

**Output 2 — The umpire's nothing-on-file confirmation.** Either Roos produces a methodology document (in which case the methodology is subpoenable and impeachable through Foster's and Tomes's depositions) or Roos confirms no document exists (in which case the *Herll* / *Rymer* "ambiguity supported by no methodology" argument is locked).

These two outputs together make the Tier 1 motion essentially a slam dunk on the present record. Everything else — Foster's disinterestedness, Tomes's non-signature, Pure Construction's scope-development chain — strengthens the motion and supports the Tier 3 reserve, but Outputs 1 and 2 alone are sufficient for Tier 1.
