# Damage Catalog — Prieve Engineering File #241008, Photo Appendix A

**Source document.** `EventDocuments/FarmersDenyLetter-11-4-AppendixA.pdf` — Photo Appendix A to Prieve Engineering File #241008, 110 pages, photographs A-1 through A-217. The captions are Prieve's own work product, created by Prieve Engineering's office in connection with the 10/9/2024 site visit at 17756 George Moran Dr.

**Why this catalog exists.** The Appraisal Award entered 9/12/2025 covered "Windows (F1.7 / B1.2)" against $12,800 RCV / $6,400 ACV. This document catalogs every observation in Appendix A that reflects damage to a covered component, grouped by elevation, so that the gap analysis in `02_AwardedScope_vs_DocumentedDamage_Gap.md` can quantify how many windows and how many non-window components Farmers' own engineer documented as damaged versus how many the panel actually evaluated and awarded.

**Methodology.** Every photograph in Appendix A whose caption describes a dent, scrape, mark, indentation, deterioration, chip, penetration, or other physical change to the window assembly, downspout, flashing, siding, trim, door, or related component is included. Photographs that are pure context shots ("VIEW OF ___") or that affirmatively show no damage ("NO DENTS OBSERVED") are noted as such but not counted as damage observations. The "Caption" column is the verbatim caption from Appendix A; the "Damage indicator" column is the family's tabulation of what the caption reports.

**Cross-reference key.**
- **In Prieve narrative?** Whether the observation is discussed in the 7-page narrative of `EventDocuments/FarmersDenyLetter-11-4.pdf` pp. 4–10.
- **F1.7 / B1.2 candidate?** Best-effort identification of whether the observation is on a window the appraisal award likely covers (based on Farmers' own descriptions in the 9/18, 9/19, 10/14/2025 emails and the family's 10/21/2025 description of "one 1-sash window at the corner of stairs facing front; and one 3-sash windows in family room facing back"). "F1.7 candidate" = front/west single-sash near stairs; "B1.2 candidate" = rear/east three-sash in family room. "Outside award" = neither.
- **In appraisal scope?** Whether the observation is within the three items the family demanded in the 8/3/2025 appraisal demand (Windows, Siding repaint, Siding R&R). Downspouts, flashing, and doors are *outside* the demanded scope and are therefore reserved coverage questions for the court regardless of the appraisal outcome.

- **B1.2** — interpreted as the rear/east three-sash window in the family room. The closest match in Appendix A is the East Family Room main 3-unit window group at A-108 / A-109 with the dents documented at A-113, A-114, A-115, A-116, A-117. **Same itemization problem.**

**That is 2 windows out of at least 19 distinct windows (plus a sliding door) Prieve documented with damage on the home.**

The damage observations at the remaining 17+ window locations — the upper-west 3-unit bedroom (A-43–46), the upper-level bedroom on the west side south of the entry (A-59–65), the upper east family room window (A-118–124), the NE-facing family room window (A-125–135), the SE window at deck (A-136–140), the north window at upper family room (A-145–146), the upper bay window group at central quadrant (A-147–160), the upper east window above porch (A-161–166), the four-season porch windows (A-167–175), the north-facing porch window (A-176–183), the kitchen corner windows (A-184–195), the main-level east NE-quadrant window (A-196–204), the upper-north window above kitchen (A-205–206), and the garage window (A-213–217) — are **the windows the appraisal panel did not evaluate** (or did not award).

Under *Quade v. Secura Insurance*, 814 N.W.2d 703 (Minn. 2012), each unevaluated window is a coverage question reserved to the court. The court is not bound by the appraisal's $0 implication on those windows; the appraisal's binding effect under § 65A.01 Subd. 3 extends only to items the panel actually appraised. (See `03_LegalFramework.md` § 3 for the legal analysis.)

## H. The Downspout / Flashing / Door-Window Issue — Reserved Coverage Questions

The 8/3/2025 Appraisal Demand listed three items: Windows, Siding (repaint), and Siding (R&R). Downspouts, gutters, flashing not attached to siding, and exterior doors were not in the demand. The panel had no jurisdiction to evaluate them.

- The 5 dented downspouts (A-42, A-92, A-93, A-98, A-99) are reserved coverage questions for the court under Count I (breach of contract).
- The 3 dented-flashing observations (A-103, A-128, A-129) are likewise reserved.
- The 1 dent on the door window screen (A-141) is reserved.

These items cannot be blocked by any "appraisal resolved the dispute" defense Farmers may raise. They were never in the appraisal. The court can hear them on a clean record under Count I, with Prieve's Photo Appendix A as the evidence base.

**The downspouts in particular are an evidentiary anchor.** The family's gutters and downspouts were replaced after 9/13/2021 (`EventDocuments/2025-01-31-ResponseToFarmer-01-31-25-StormsAfter2020.pdf`). Any dent on those components must post-date late 2021. Prieve documented 5 such dents in October 2024. That is unambiguous physical proof of a post-2020 hail event at the property — the very fact Prieve denies in his Weather Records and Conclusion 1.
