## Notes on Authentication

### A. Public Records (Exhibits 4, 10, 19, 20)

National Weather Service records and Minnesota state and municipal records are self-authenticating under Minn. R. Evid. 902(1) (domestic public documents) and 902(5) (official publications). The NWS storm report can be admitted without custodian testimony. City of Eden Prairie permits may require a Rule 902(11) certification by the City's records custodian.

### B. Party-Opponent Admissions (Exhibits 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 13, 15)

Documents produced by Defendant in discovery and authored by Defendant or its agents are admissible as party-opponent admissions under Minn. R. Evid. 801(d)(2)(D). No additional authentication is required.

### C. Sworn Transcripts (Exhibits 7, 14, 17)

Deposition transcripts authenticated by the court reporter's certificate and the affidavit's verification under oath. Routine.

### D. Commercial Weather-Data Records (Exhibits 11, 16)

Commercial database records (Stormersite, Interactive Hail Maps) require:
- Custodian affidavit under Minn. R. Evid. 803(6) (business records exception); or
- Rule 902(11) certification by the data provider; or
- Stipulated authenticity through pretrial conference.

For the Motion, the family can rely on the Stormersite and Interactive Hail Maps records as cited in Plaintiffs' 1/31/2025 and 2/10/2025 submissions to Farmers (Exhibits 9 and 16) — those submissions were received and reviewed (or refused) by Farmers, which itself establishes the authenticity for the limited purpose of disproving Prieve's premise.

### E. Plaintiff's Communication Log (Exhibit 12)

Family's contemporaneous business record. Admissible under Minn. R. Evid. 803(6) (business records exception, as Plaintiffs' regular record of claim-related communications) or 803(1) (present-sense impression for individual entries). A foundation affidavit from Plaintiffs may be necessary.

## Exhibit Pre-Filing Checklist

Before serving and filing the Motion:

- [ ] Each exhibit Bates-stamped consistently.
- [ ] Each exhibit cross-referenced to a specific paragraph or page in the Memorandum in Support.
- [ ] Each exhibit cross-referenced to a specific paragraph or page in the Expert Affidavit.
- [ ] Custodian affidavits prepared for Exhibits 11 and 16 (or stipulated authenticity arranged).
- [ ] Subpoena returns or related production records archived for Exhibits 5, 8, 10, 13, 14.
- [ ] NWS storm report (Exhibit 4) verified against the Eden Prairie ASOS station data table at the time of filing.
- [ ] City of Eden Prairie permits (Exhibit 10) verified against current City records (may require updated certified copies).
- [ ] HailShapes literature compilation (Exhibit 18) verified for peer-reviewed status of each cited article.
- [ ] Exhibit binder prepared in two formats: (a) Bates-stamped paper for filing; (b) PDF for e-filing.

## Notes on Sealing and Confidentiality

Some exhibits may be subject to confidentiality designations from the protective order:

- **Exhibit 5 (Prieve Engagement Record)** — may be designated "Confidential" by Defendant; if so, file under seal per the operative protective order.
- **Exhibit 14 (Brau Deposition Transcript)** — may contain portions designated "Confidential"; if so, redact or file under seal.
- All other exhibits are public-record or are otherwise non-confidential.

If any exhibit is sealed, the Motion's public version should reference the exhibit's sealed status and the Memorandum should cite the sealed version under the appropriate protective-order procedure.

## Coordination With the § 604.18 Subd. 4(a) Motion (If Filed Concurrently)

If the Daubert motion is filed concurrently with the § 604.18 Subd. 4(a) motion to amend, the exhibit lists overlap substantially. Coordinate exhibit numbering between the two motions or use a unified exhibit set with cross-references in both motions.

A unified exhibit set is generally preferred — avoids redundant filing and lets the court see the full record at one hearing.
